A6000-01 · Inert-gas identity — argon or nitrogen

No basis approved — MoC on DBM §3.6.3 / PFD-6000

Reasoning: The gas identity decides the source (cryogenic argon vs nitrogen generator or bulk N₂), the purity chain and the demand table for every user.

Observation: D-A6000-01 records the conflict and approves no basis: argon on PFD-6000, DAT-004 / DAT-008, MEB-009 / 010 and in the model (AR-701, HD-6201, bulk TK-6101 configuration) versus nitrogen in DBM §3.6.3; R-201 blanketing needs N₂ (NTE-010) while the furnaces need argon. The one approved value is the furnace argon specification — ≥ 99.999 % with ≤ 2 ppm O₂ at source (DAT-004 / DAT-008, REP-043). No demand has been tabulated.

Required action: Rule the plant inert-gas basis by MoC on DBM §3.6.3 / PFD-6000 — argon for A-700 / A-800 / A-900 at ≥ 99.999 %, nitrogen for A-200 blanketing (N2-HEADER-201) — then tabulate demand (AR-701 / 702, AR-801 / 802, blanketing) and size the A-6100 supply package.

Evidence: D-A6000-01 GAP-A-6000-DF-f1d9ef GAP-A-6000-ID-f21cdd A-6100 FEED-PE-REP-043

A6000-02 · Register packages vs modelled source equipment

Register reconciliation required

Reasoning: The register lists two packages; the model lists the equipment inside them — one identity scheme is needed before values can attach.

Observation: LST-001 lists A-6100 (inert-gas supply) and A-6200 (distribution) as packages with no model record; the model owns TK-6101 liquid-argon storage, E-6101 ambient vaporizer, MN-6102 reserve cylinder bank and the PCV-6101 / PCV-6102 regulation and changeover station under the bulk-argon configuration, with fire point FE-6001 at the source approach — none of these has a register tag or a semantic record. Materials are ruled for both packages (MAT-002: 316L bright-annealed / electropolished tubing and regulator bodies; MDL-001 304L superseded).

Required action: Either register the model source equipment under A-6100 / A-6200 (with the alternative source configuration recorded) or model the packages as single records; then apply the MAT-002 rulings to the equipment.

Evidence: GAP-A-6000-ID-e742af A-6100 A-6200 D-A6000-MOC CF-materialOfConstruc-A-6100 FEED-PE-MAT-002 FEED-PE-LST-001

A6000-03 · Distribution header and user manifolds

Modelled — demand and reservations open

Reasoning: The header and manifolds are sized on the tabulated demand and carry the A-900 reservations that are currently blinded.

Observation: The model provides the common distribution header HD-6201 (1,415 parts) and the A-700 purge / process manifold AR-701 (1,694 parts), both with semantic records but no register identity; the A-800 manifold AR-801 sits in A-800; the three A-900 takeoffs from HD-6201 are positively blinded reservations (S-900-901 … 903). The emergency-water source and run-off interfaces are semantic boundaries with unresolved physical tags.

Required action: Size HD-6201 and the manifolds on the demand table once the gas basis is ruled; register HD-6201 / AR-701 / AR-801 on LST-001; keep the A-900 branches blinded until that area is reconciled.

Evidence: HD-6201 AR-701 IF-A6000-EW-SOURCE IF-A6000-EW-RUNOFF MODEL
