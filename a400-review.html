Findings

A400-01 · Membrane technology — hold H-01

Technology hold — model shows one candidate

Reasoning: Every sizing value in the area (flux, area, module count, pumps, vessels, water) follows the membrane technology; none can be frozen before it.

Observation: Three configurations are on record: VSEP i84 vibratory-shear (REP-001 V1.1, the MEB-007 model and the 3D geometry — eight 84-inch packs), a standalone spiral-wound TFF reference case (REP-007: 238 Synder elements, 981 m², rejected as procurement basis) and a hybrid bulk mechanical separation + TFF polishing (REP-017, provisional). MAT-002 M-029 (ceramic) and CAL-010 (PES/PVDF hollow fibre) assume different membranes again. D-A400-01 approves none and keeps the model class generic; the modelled VSEP is a candidate, not a selection.

Required action: Run the H-01 bench program on actual A-300 quench cake at pH 2–4 (flux, c_max, fouling), select the configuration, then re-issue FEED-PE-MEB-007 (V18) on the DBM V2.0 basis and re-tag the model package accordingly.

A400-02 · Graphite and wash-water basis

Basis withdrawn — 8× spread in wash water

Reasoning: Diafiltration duty scales with graphite rate and with the wash-water ratio; both must be single-valued before the package is sized.

Observation: MEB-007 runs on 50 kg/h graphite (CAL-010 is a 10 kg pilot batch) against the DBM V2.0 design rate of 63.5 kg/h; wash water is 887.5 kg/kg graphite as equivalent diavolumes (MEB-007), 7,088 kg/kg in the pilot (CAL-010) and 7,691 kg/kg in the cost model (REP-017). REP-036 shows the pilot excess is cycle geometry and surface buffer, not chemistry. D-A400-01 withdrew flux (72 / 78 LMH), diavolumes (36.8 / 37.0 / 284), module count (8 / 1), permeate duty and the T-402 / T-403 volumes; the 20 m³ and 98.6 m³ figures are screening or pilot inputs.

Required action: Fix one graphite rate (DBM V2.0 Table 3.3-1) and one wash-water basis from the H-01 bench results; then size TFF-401, T-402, T-403 and the wastewater case with A-1000 / A-2000 (REP-038).

A400-03 · Wash endpoint and product-release criterion

Approved endpoints — two targets to reconcile

Reasoning: The wash endpoint decides batch time, water consumption and the interface to QA release.

Observation: Approved: feed pH 1 from the A-300 acid wash and target pH 5 washed product (D-A400-02, MEB-007; all documents agree); PFD-0400 endpoint "permeate conductivity < 100–200 µS/cm and pH ≈ 5"; REP-001 Table 5 wash-liquor target pH 5.0 ± 0.2, SO₄²⁻ ≤ 1 mg/L, Mn non-detect, conductivity < 10 µS/cm; low-TMP operating philosophy; membrane limits 25 °C at pH < 2 / 50 °C above (polymeric options). The two conductivity targets differ by an order of magnitude and REP-036 shows pH 5 and SO₄ ≤ 1 mg/L are not equivalent once the surface buffer dominates.

Required action: Decide whether pH, conductivity or sulphate is the release criterion, reconcile PFD-0400 with REP-001 and link the result to FEED-QA-SPC-001; carry the chosen endpoint into the LST-007 loops and the C&E matrix.

Evidence: D-A400-02 GAP-A-400-DF-6c25b2 FEED-PE-PFD-001 FEED-PE-REP-001 FEED-PE-REP-036

A400-04 · Pump duties P-402 / P-403 / P-405

Duties withdrawn — configuration-dependent

Reasoning: Circulation and permeate pump duties are set by the membrane configuration and the wash-water basis.

Observation: P-402 2,838 m³/h and 363.8 kW belong to the standalone-TFF reference case (REP-007), rejected as procurement basis by REP-017; MEB-007 gives 229.5 kW shaft on the 50 / 80 kg/h basis. In MEB-007 P-405 is an RO concentrate pump, whereas the model P-405 is the CIP circulation pump of CIP-401 with tank TK-405 — same tag, different service. D-A400-01 withdrew all three duties.

Required action: Re-derive pump duties from the re-issued MEB-007; resolve the P-405 identity (RO concentrate vs CIP circulation) on PFD-0400 and LST-001 before datasheets.

A400-05 · Materials of construction

Ruled — confirm with the H-01 selection

Observation: D-A400-MOC applies the plant-wide ruling that FEED-PE-MAT-002 governs: T-402 PVDF- or PFA-lined carbon-steel pressure vessel (M-031), TFF-401 PVDF / PP-lined or dual-laminate FRP, P-402 PVDF / ETFE-lined sealless with SiC bearings, T-403 / P-403 HDPE / PP / PVDF / dual-laminate FRP. Destinations open

Reasoning: Each vent and drain needs an identified destination compatible with the liquor it carries.

Observation: The model carries five interface records — IF-A400-T402-VENT, IF-A400-T403-VENT, IF-A400-TFF-VENT, IF-A400-DRAIN, IF-A400-TFF-DRAIN — without register identities or destinations. A400-08 · Provisional H-01 baseline — hybrid washing and contaminant endpoint

Provisional baseline approved (D-A400-03) — H-01 open

Reasoning: FEED-PE-REP-036 showed that the basis every A-400 size was derived from — slurry pH 5 reached by water — needs 500–2,000 m³/h commercially and is not feasible; without a provisional basis A-400 could carry no sizing values or budget datasheets.

Observation: FEED-PE-NTE-013 V1.0 assesses the three configurations of A400-01 against REP-001, REP-007, REP-017 and REP-036 and records the baseline approved in review (H1–H5). H1: hybrid — counter-current displacement wash of a filterable cake at pH < 3 to Mn / K non-detect, re-slurry, membrane polish (TFF-401) and RO-401 recovery; standalone TFF rejected, VSEP retained only as a polish-membrane format; separator type open. H2: endpoint on contaminants with the buffer-signature stop rule, slurry pH monitored (3.5–4.2 expected), pH ≈ 5 only by neutralisation if A-500 needs it. H3: DBM V2.0 63.5 kg/h graphite, 91.7 kg/h TLGO. H4: 2.6–6.9 m³/h wash water (41–109 kg/kg graphite) against 71 m³/h in REP-001 / 007. D-A400-03 approves the technology baseline and the wash endpoint from NTE-013 — closing the technology-baseline and wash-endpoint conflicts and superseding the PFD pH ≈ 5 endpoint of D-A400-02 — and keeps the REP-001 contaminant targets with the pH element monitored. The water basis and rate enter as candidates; the D-A400-01 withdrawals stand. This answers the endpoint question of A400-03 (its D-A400-02 follow-up can be closed at the next data-layer ruling) and gives A400-01 / A400-02 a provisional basis, not a selection.

Required action: Run M1 (titration), M3 (IC / ICP), M4 (cake displacement test) and M8 (flux) with the re-ruling triggers of NTE-013 §8; raise the MoC for the DBM A-400 requirement; re-issue REP-001 / REP-007 and MEB-007 V18; reconcile the A-300 separator (C-301 decanter vs the filter press assumed by REP-036); then issue the A-400 budget datasheets on H3 / H4.

A400-09 · Equipment datasheets on the provisional baseline — T-402 in two options

Preliminary datasheets issued — T-402 pressure mode open

Reasoning: With the provisional H-01 baseline (A400-08) the polish loop could be sized; T-402 had no design conditions, P-402 / P-403 no type or head, and MAT-002 M-031 writes T-402 as a 60 psi pressure-assisted vessel while the model shows a closed-vented tank.

Observation: FEED-PE-CAL-022 V1.0 sizes the loop on the bases approved in review (T1–T6): 91.7 kg/h TLGO → 82.5 kg/h at 1 wt % = 8.2 m³/h to T-501 (D-A500-02), continuous constant-level loop. FEED-PE-DAT-053: T-402 8.2 m³ working / 11 m³ in two options for comparison — A atmospheric closed-vented (+50 / −5 mbarg, ≈ 1.6 t, pump-set TMP) and B 60 psi pressure-assisted per MAT-002 M-031 (5.0 barg / full vacuum, ASME VIII, 8 mm governed by vacuum, ≈ 1.9 t, PSV, ≈ 11 Nm³ air per pressurisation, feed and make-up pumped against ≈ 42 m); steel mass is similar, the difference is code, relief, liner vacuum rating and the loop around it. FEED-PE-DAT-054: TFF-401 6.9 m³/h permeate, 173 m² required at 40 LMH, 220 m² installed, format open. FEED-PE-DAT-055: T-403 18 m³. FEED-PE-DAT-056 / 057: P-402 9 m³/h × 18 m at 1,000 mPa·s (low-shear), P-403 7.6 m³/h × 13 m. 114 candidate rows, none approved; nine A-400 missing-value gaps close. The two T-402 design pressures are carried as an open conflict so the choice is visible; datasheet values whose only contenders were values withdrawn by D-A400-01 are not counted as conflicts (opt-in harvester rule, no other area affected). RO-401, T-404 / P-404 / T-405 and the CIP-401 items remain unsized (identity holds).

Required action: Select the T-402 pressure mode by decision (hold W6; Option A needs an MoC on the MAT-002 M-031 wording); measure slurry rheology (W2) and flux (W1, M8); register RO-401 and resolve the CIP-401 / MEB-007 tag conflict (W4) before sizing them.

A400-10 · T-402 pressure mode — Option A atmospheric selected

Decided (D-A400-04) — MoC and DAT-053 V1.1 to follow

Reasoning: FEED-PE-DAT-053 carried T-402 in two options (A400-09) so the pressure mode could be chosen on a like-for-like comparison; the choice sets the vessel code, relief, liner rating, compressed air and the head of every pump feeding the loop.

Observation: D-A400-04 (QFL, 2026-09-24) selects Option A: an atmospheric closed-vented tank at +50 / −5 mbarg with a pump-driven loop, TMP set by the retentate back-pressure valve. FEED-PE-CAL-022 showed similar steel mass (≈ 1.6 t against ≈ 1.9 t); Option B would have added ASME VIII certification and radiography, a PSV, a vacuum-rated liner, ≈ 11 Nm³ of air per pressurisation and ≈ 42 m on the feed and make-up pumps. The decision approves the design pressure from DAT-053 item 4.1 and supersedes item 5.1 (a new optional approvedLocation field narrows an approval to one item where one source carries options); the T-402 design-pressure conflict is closed. MAT-002 M-031 still describes T-402 as pressure-assisted to 60 psi.

Required action: Raise the MoC on FEED-PE-MAT-002 M-031 (pressure-assisted → atmospheric closed-vented; lined CS or dual-laminate FRP) and issue FEED-PE-DAT-053 V1.1 with Option A as the design; keep TMP control and the retentate back-pressure valve in the TFF-401 enquiry.
