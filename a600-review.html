A600-01 · Dryer readiness — twelve REP-016 holds

NOT READY for vendor inquiry

Reasoning: A spray dryer cannot be sized or enquired before atomisation, feed rheology, the heat and mass balance, product temperature limits and the dust basis are settled.

Observation: D-A600-03 records the REP-016 V1.4 readiness statement as controlling: DR-601 is NOT READY, twelve high-priority HOLDs open (HOLD-1…8 on the dryer: atomizer, rheology / solids, H&M balance, moisture and product temperature, heating medium and HX-601, dust, LEL, explosion protection; HOLD-9 F-601 sizing; HOLD-10 SC-601 sizing). D-A600-01 withdrew every MEB-008 V6 rate — evaporation, drying air, feed 50 kg/h graphite, product 27.8 kg/h (about half the DBM V2.0 nominal of 57 kg/h), the cyclone / bottom / baghouse recovery split, the 3,840 kW HX-601 duty and the BL-601 / FN-601 / P-601 powers. The modelled tower, filter house and scrubber are layout envelopes.

Required action: Close HOLD-1…8, then re-issue MEB-008 (V7) on DBM V2.0 (63.5 kg/h graphite; ≈ 57–74 kg/h dried TLGO) with the recovery train as drawn; size F-601 and SC-601 (HOLD-9 / 10) on that balance before any vendor enquiry.

A600-02 · Drying envelope and feed solids

Targets approved — subject to HOLD-4 / HOLD-1

Reasoning: Inlet and outlet air temperatures, residual moisture and feed solids define the dryer duty and the product quality.

Observation: Approved as current design targets (D-A600-02, single source MEB-008): 140 °C air inlet, 85 °C outlet, 2 wt% residual moisture — each subject to the HOLD-4 product-temperature and final-moisture limits. Feed solids ≈ 1 wt% is approved with the REP-025 reasoning that it is a rheological / atomisation ceiling (GO nematic gel by ~1 wt%), so upstream mechanical dewatering (REP-017 / REP-036, about 5 wt% from A-400) is the lever; MEB-008 carries the same 0.01 number. HX-601 duty ≈ 3.8 MW at 1 wt% is the dominant A-600 utility and its heating medium is HOLD-5.

Required action: Test atomisation of TLGO slurry above 1 wt% (HOLD-1 / HOLD-2) and decide the A-400 → A-600 concentration basis jointly with D-A400-01 and D-A500-02; fix the HX-601 heating medium with the A-5000 review (≥ 200 °C loop).

Evidence: D-A600-02 D-A600-03 GAP-A-600-DF-0dcaec FEED-PE-REP-025 FEED-PE-MEB-008

A600-03 · Combustible dust, LEL and explosion protection

Intent approved — assessment open (HOLD-6…8)

Reasoning: Dried GO powder in the dryer, bag filter and receiver is a combustible-dust service; the protection basis must precede equipment selection.

Observation: D-A600-03 adopts the REP-016 HOLD-6…8 requirements as safeguard intent for DR-601, F-601 and T-602 — combustible dust hazard assessment, solvent vapour / LEL monitoring, explosion protection — with MAT-002 M-040 conductive, grounded PTFE filter media. The model shows the recovery train (F-601 → FN-601 → SC-601) and the contained receiver T-602 at the dryer cone; no venting, suppression or isolation devices are represented.

Required action: Complete the dust hazard assessment (Kst, MIE, MIT on dried TLGO), select explosion protection and isolation for DR-601 / F-601 / T-602, define LEL monitoring, and carry the result into the P&ID and the datasheets.

Evidence: D-A600-03 DR-601 F-601 T-602 FEED-PE-REP-016 FEED-PE-MAT-002

A600-04 · Materials of construction

Ruled — F-601 and T-602 not yet covered

Reasoning: Product-contact surfaces of the dryer, heater and recovery train set the metal budget of the dried GO; the wet scrubber sees acidic condensate.

Observation: D-A600-MOC applies MAT-002: 316L / 321 electropolished product-contact surfaces (Ra ≤ 0.8 µm) for DR-601, HX-601 and BL-601; PVDF / PTFE-lined lines with PVDF-lined or 316L pump and tungsten-carbide or ceramic nozzle for T-601 / P-601; vinyl-ester FRP scrubber with PP / PVDF packing for SC-601 and FN-601. MDL-001 (HDPE / XLPE tank, 304L casings, OEM aluminium blower) superseded. F-601 and T-602 have an MDL-001 material only and remain candidate until MAT-002 covers them.

Required action: Extend MAT-002 to F-601 (housing and media) and T-602; issue the A-600 datasheets on the MAT-002 base case once HOLD-1…8 close.

A600-05 · Register and model reconciliation

Register reconciliation required

Reasoning: Every equipment item in the area must have one identity in the register, the model and the data layer.

Observation: All nine LST-001 tags (T-601, P-601, DR-601, BL-601, HX-601, F-601, FN-601, SC-601, T-602) are bound to model geometry. The model also owns the scrubber recirculation pump P-SC601, the battery limit BL-A600, the stair tower PL-601, two fire points and three emergency stations without register identities. Two items named in design notes are absent from PFD-0600, LST-001 and the model: cyclone CY-601 (the PFD routes the dryer discharge directly to the F-601 bag-filter house) and blower B-601 (the register calls it BL-601). Fifteen interface records (gas, exhaust, water, blowdown, CIP supply / return, dryer and filter wash and vents, feed and scrubber pump drains) have no destinations.

Required action: Register P-SC601 and BL-A600 on LST-001; rule CY-601 in or out with the MEB-008 V7 recovery split; align the B-601 / BL-601 name; assign destinations to the fifteen interfaces on PFD-0600 (A-3000 for exhaust, A-1000 for wash and blowdown).

A600-06 · Access, maintenance and fire points

Model access provided — qualification open

Reasoning: A dryer tower and bag-filter house need rated access for nozzle, bag and cone maintenance, with fire points at the approaches.

Observation: The model provides a dedicated dryer / filter access stair tower PL-601 (1,166 parts) and places fire points at the dryer stair approach (FE-601) and the dryer feed approach (FE-602), with three emergency stations ES-601 … ES-603. Platform loads, bag-change and atomizer-removal routes, and the T-602 receiver change-out are not qualified by this review; wash interfaces (dryer wash, filter wash, wash collection) exist as records only.

Required action: Qualify the PL-601 tower against the atomizer, bag and cone maintenance tasks (lifted masses, laydown, hoist), confirm fire-point and station positions in the plant-wide access and fire-safety reviews, and define the wash-down and wash-collection routes.

A600-07 · MEB-008 V8.0 — A-600 re-based to DBM V2.0

Candidates carried — sizing still withdrawn; datasheets with the spray-drying session

Reasoning: The A-600 balance was on 50 kg/h graphite while every issued area uses the DBM V2.0 design rate; the data layer could no longer be harvested because MEB-008 V6.0 had been archived.

Observation: FEED-PE-MEB-008 V8.0 (register V4.0) re-bases A-600 to DBM V2.0 design: 63.46 kg/h graphite → 82.48 kg/h TLGO at 1 wt % (8,248 kg/h slurry, the FEED-PE-CAL-023 interface), 8,164 kg/h evaporation, 382,868 kg/h dry air, HX-601 12.52 MW, BL-601 652 kW and FN-601 486 kW shaft, product 74.2 kg/h (MEB-008 symbol) — about 33 % above V7.2. The data layer now reads V8.0: its values enter as candidates after all earlier rows (no renumbering; the V6.0 rows keep their V6.0 label, read from the archived file), the D-A600-01 withdrawals stand, and the approved drying targets (140 / 85 °C, 2 wt %) and the 1 wt % feed are unchanged in V8.0, so they are not re-harvested. CFD-002 V5.0, REP-044 V1.2 and NTE-012 V1.1 are listed as sources (REP-044 and NTE-012 still carry 50 kg/h numbers). New open gap: DBM V2.0 treats the A-600 factor 0.90 as a physical baghouse / wall loss, MEB-008 applies 0.90 as a chemical loss plus f_carry 0.880 as wall loss — overall ≈ 0.79, most of the ×1.3 margin (MEB-008 Assumption #26). The A-600 datasheets are assigned by QFL to the spray-drying session (CAL-025, DAT-063 onward).

Required action: Rule the A-600 solids-factor basis (Assumption #26); issue the A-600 datasheets from MEB-008 V8.0 (spray-drying session) and harvest them late; re-scale CFD-002 V5.1 to 383 t/h air and revise REP-044 / NTE-012 to V8.0.
