A200-01 · Bank allocation — four dedicated packages vs the register

Register reconciliation required

Reasoning: Each reactor needs an explicitly assigned feed, circulation and transfer arrangement.

Observation: The model allocates one Pre-G dosing, one KMnO₄ dosing, one powder-induction package and one recirculation pump to each of the four reactors (H-201A–D / SF-201A–D, H-202A–D / SF-202A–D, SI-201A–D / M-201A–D, P-205A–D) and one selectable transfer pair P-206A / B; LST-001 lists single H-201, H-202, SF-201, SF-202 and M-201 and P-205A / B without assigning them to four reactors. The data layer records the compound model tags as "via composite" register matches and the single register tags as not in the model; P-205C / D and the SI-201 induction packages have no register identity. DBM V2.0 sizes the bank as four reactors on a 24 h cycle staggered 6 h.

Required action: Reconcile the four-package model with LST-001 and the P&ID — shared versus dedicated dosing, induction and recirculation, and the P-206 duty / standby basis — and add the per-reactor tags or the aliases by decision.

A200-02 · Feed and dosing — ratios approved, KMnO₄ route open

Ratio basis approved — addition method unresolved

Reasoning: Dosing requires a known ratio basis, a defined addition route and a permissive basis.

Observation: D-A200-02 approves the PFD V5.1.1 denominator: 66.24 kg H₂SO₄, 6.75 kg H₃PO₄ and 4 kg KMnO₄ per kg Pre-G, carried by LST-001, LST-007, DAT-003 and MDL-001; NTE-008's per-kg-graphite ratios are superseded, with the pilot 46 kg/kg question (NTE-008 §9.2 Q6) left as a follow-up. The KMnO₄ addition method is an open conflict across all four lines — NTE-004 Option E pneumatic surface-impingement, NTE-007 Venturi eductor VE-201 with rotary valve, LST-001 twin-screw LIW feeder SF-202 → M-201 static mixer — and the model shows the LIW-feeder-plus-induction arrangement. Streams 203 / 207 and 212 / 213 are recorded as identity items; VE-201, XV-201 and DC-201 exist only in design notes.

Required action: Verify the 66.24 kg/kg ratio against the pilot batch record (MoC on PFD-001 if the pilot recipe is the true one); decide the KMnO₄ addition method and fix the C&E permissives (agitation, temperature, N₂ blanket) before the P&ID.

A200-03 · Recirculation, sampling and transfer

Duties open — stream 216 double use

Reasoning: Sampling and transfer must not inadvertently open product, waste or recovery paths; pump duties need the batch basis.

Observation: The model provides a recirculation pump per reactor (P-205A–D, owned by the reactor records) and the selectable transfer pair P-206A / B. LST-001 reuses stream 216 for both the recirculation take-off and the TLGO transfer to A-300 (flagged); P-205 / P-206 duties are unapproved and depend on the withdrawn batch basis. Transfer temperature 10 °C is approved (D-A200-04: PFD "chill down to 10 °C", pilot-verified in NTE-008; DAT-003 ≤ 20 °C and NTE-010 ≈ 30 °C superseded). The V119 sampling-scenario simulation is not re-run here.

Required action: Split stream 216 on LST-001; derive P-205 / P-206 duties from CAL-011 V8; verify sample-receiver arrangements, valve fail positions and independent protection on the P&ID.

A200-04 · Jacket and thermal interfaces

Duty withdrawn — coolant supply conflict open

Reasoning: The connected utility must remove the peak reaction heat across the jacket; the jacket area and coolant temperature decide whether it can.

Observation: D-A200-01 withdrew the peak cooling duty (629 kW DAT-003 / 590 kW CAL-011 V7), the jacket area required and available and the adequacy ratio — all derived from the withdrawn batch mass — noting that the "jacket insufficient" conclusion is expected to hold or worsen on the DBM V2.0 basis. The jacket coolant supply is an open conflict: 5 °C chilled water during KMnO₄ addition (NTE-008) vs ≈ 15 °C U-201 / ≈ 25 °C (NTE-010); the A-5000 review carries the chilled-water and exchanger question. The four jacket subassemblies are model-only.

Required action: Complete CAL-011 V8 on the DBM V2.0 basis (batch ≈ 42.9 t), then fix the peak duty, jacket adequacy and the coolant supply (chilled vs cooling water) with the A-5000 utility ruling; run reaction calorimetry (A-059).

A200-05 · Top drive, impeller and maintenance

Impeller type in conflict — disassembly basis open

Reasoning: The agitator type sets the drive, the removal envelope and the mixing / suspension basis; maintenance must provide a physically achievable removal sequence.

Observation: The impeller type is an open conflict on all four reactors — retreat-curve impeller (DAT-003) vs close-clearance anchor / helical (NTE-008) — as is the bottom head (2:1 semi-elliptical with bottom outlet, DAT-003, vs conical ≥ 60°, NTE-008). The model reactor carries a top drive and head nozzles; the V119 mechanical basis recorded that the impeller envelope exceeds the cover neck bore and that split-impeller retrieval must be qualified. Vessel design temperature 80 °C and design pressure +50 mbarg / full vacuum are approved from DAT-003 (D-A200-05 / -07; the uncontrolled RFQ 3.0 barg is not adopted).

Required action: Decide the impeller and bottom-head type with the CAL-011 V8 sizing; obtain the vendor disassembly procedure, lifted masses and rigging; confirm the relief basis before freezing the design pressure (DAT-003 15.8).

A200-06 · Drainage and containment

Receiving and retention basis open

Reasoning: Residual process liquid needs an accessible, defined receiving path and an assessed capture arrangement sized to the real batch.

Observation: The model owns independent reactor bunds BND-201A–D (21.4 m³ retention each) and the acid-tank bunds BND-202 (39.9 m³) / BND-203 (18.9 m³), two fire points and seven emergency stations, none on LST-001. The retention figures predate the DBM V2.0 batch (≈ 42.9 t per reactor); the bottom-outlet arrangement and closed-drain destination are model conventions, not verified against a receiving-system design, and the T-203 receiver and E-201 exchanger named in design notes are in neither register nor model.

Required action: Re-derive bund retention from the DBM V2.0 batch inventory; verify residual heel, dead legs, isolation and the closed-drain destination; rule T-203 / E-201 in or out.

A200-07 · Vent, purge and relief interfaces

Envelope and ladder approved — relief and destination open

Reasoning: Each gas and pressure interface needs an identified function, destination and design basis; the temperature ladder is the primary safeguard of an exothermic permanganate oxidation.

Observation: D-A200-03 approves the temperature envelope and ladder — 47.5 °C setpoint, 45–50 °C allowable, TALL 43 / TAL 46 / TAH 49 / TAHH 50 °C (SIS) per NTE-003, consistent with PFD Safety Note 1 (≤ 49 °C) and DAT-003; NTE-010's ≈ 50 / ≈ 55 °C stages are superseded (55 °C is the non-recoverable Mn₂O₇ point of NTE-007). Nitrogen blanketing comes from the model header N2-HEADER-201 and the battery limit BL-A200; the inert-gas identity itself is open in A-6000 (D-A6000-01). Relief sizing, vent capacity and the destination in A-3000 are not established; design pressure is subject to the relief case.

Required action: Correct NTE-010 §11 to the NTE-003 ladder before the P&ID; confirm SIL and 2oo3 architecture in LOPA; reconcile nozzle and line tags to the P&ID and fix the relief basis, vent destination (A-3000 S1) and purge demand.
