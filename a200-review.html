A-200 · Review v119 · 2026-09-14

A-200 assembly & design review

Four-reactor oxidation bank: equipment allocation, dosing, recirculation, thermal interfaces and maintainability.

Concept model assessment. No physical changes or operating release. Geometry and simulation checks do not establish process safety, pressure ratings or capacity.

What this review establishes

Priority finding: Four reactors follow the V5.1 bank. Four dedicated recirculation / induction and solids-metering packages are a proposed configuration: the register lists P-205A/B and M-201 without assigning them to four reactors. P-206A/B are selectable transfer pumps; duty/standby and simultaneous operation are unconfirmed. No rated capacity or operating setpoint is implied.

Model checks passed: 80 sampling scenarios, 16 positive maintenance closures and operating-position restoration for four feeder branches. These checks cover the existing illustrated configurations only; full connectivity, nozzle alignment, clearance, lifting and process-performance acceptance are not established.

Image basis: 26,644 parts assigned to A-200 by the current engineering register. Images are CPU projections of actual assembled meshes, with no explosion or sectioning. Close-ups isolate named equipment owners and may omit shared infrastructure. An open-looking nozzle in an isolated image does not by itself establish a disconnected model route. Material colours are not process temperatures.

Assembly evidence and findings

A200-01 · Bank allocation

Register reconciliation required

Reasoning: Each reactor needs an explicitly assigned feed, circulation and transfer arrangement.

Observation: Four reactors follow the V5.1 bank. Four dedicated recirculation / induction and solids-metering packages are a proposed configuration: the register lists P-205A/B and M-201 without assigning them to four reactors. P-206A/B are selectable transfer pumps; duty/standby and simultaneous operation are unconfirmed. No rated capacity or operating setpoint is implied.

Required action: Reconcile the four-package model with the equipment register and P&ID; establish shared versus dedicated duties and simultaneous operation.

A200-02 · Feed and dosing

Operating basis open

Reasoning: Dosing requires a known source, destination and permissive basis.

Observation: The model includes separate solids-metering and induction assemblies. Their existence does not establish allowable addition rate or reaction control.

Required action: Confirm feed properties, dosing envelope, calorimetry, mixing/recirculation requirements and the cause-and-effect matrix.

A200-03 · Recirculation and sampling

Limited model checks passed

Reasoning: Sampling must not inadvertently open product, waste or recovery paths.

Observation: 80 sampling scenarios passed the existing model test. Main product, cleaning-waste and recovery valves remained closed in those cases. This is simulation evidence, not commissioned protection.

Required action: Verify pump duty, suction conditions, line sizing, actual sample receiver arrangements, valve fail positions and independent protection requirements.

A200-04 · Jacket and thermal interfaces

Thermal qualification open

Reasoning: The connected utility must remove the required heat across the jacket and any intermediate exchanger.

Observation: The model represents jacket connections. The A-5000 review retains unresolved secondary temperatures and exchanger performance; this review performs no new heat-duty calculation.

Required action: Complete the batch heat balance and reaction calorimetry; establish secondary temperatures, circulation, exchanger duty and cooling-loss response.

A200-05 · Top drive and maintenance

Disassembly and lifting hold

Reasoning: Maintenance must provide a physically achievable removal sequence with rated lifting equipment.

Observation: The mechanical basis records that the impeller envelope exceeds the cover neck bore. Split-impeller disassembly and retrieval must be qualified. Tests confirmed 16 modeled positive maintenance closures and restoration of all four feeder branches to their operating positions.

Required action: Obtain the vendor disassembly procedure, task access, lifted masses, rigging, support capacity and service-disconnection sequence.

A200-06 · Drainage and containment

Receiving and retention basis open

Reasoning: Residual process liquid needs an accessible, defined receiving path and an assessed capture arrangement.

Observation: The central seat meets the inner head low point. A lowering poppet opens into a side-discharge chamber; the actuator remains below the chamber, outside the liquid path. Downstream drain routing falls to the existing closed-drain interface. Residual heel, valve internals and cleanability require vendor confirmation. This review does not recalculate containment capacity or prove chemical compatibility.

Required action: Verify residual heel, dead legs, destination, isolation, receiving capacity and the applicable containment inventory. Review surrounding containment in the full plant.

A200-07 · Vent, purge and relief interfaces

Pressure and destination review open

Reasoning: Each gas and pressure interface needs an identified function, destination and design basis.

Observation: Attached service geometry can be inspected in the assembly image. This assessment has not verified relief sizing, vent capacity, purge demand or compatibility of combined sources.

Required action: Reconcile nozzle and line tags to the P&ID; confirm normal vent and relief destinations, backpressure, purge basis and source compatibility.
