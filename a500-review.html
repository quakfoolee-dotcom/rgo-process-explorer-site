Findings

A500-01 · Sonication acceptance criterion and dose window

Not defined — gating item for A-500

Reasoning: Without a measurable acceptance criterion no dose, pass count, acoustic power, loop flow or cooler duty can be set.

Observation: REP-024 records the acceptance criterion as NOT DEFINED (RED): "fully sonicated" must be replaced by a layer-count / thickness (AFM), lateral-size PSD, viscosity or turbidity proxy. D-A500-01 therefore withdrew cumulative dose, dose per pass, pass count (N ≈ 3–5 illustrative), slurry flow, specific energy, residence time and acoustic power (29–573 kW across the plausible window); the HX-501 duty equals installed acoustic power and cannot be fixed either. D-A500-03 approved the requirement itself and the technology benchmark (HPH, rotor-stator, no treatment at equal kJ/kg) before the unit count is frozen.

Required action: Run the REP-024 §12 bridging program on TLGO at design concentration — dose–response map, acceptance criterion and dose window (D_total, D_v, N), loop RTD test, technology benchmark — then size US-501, P-501 and HX-501 and close A-500 by MoC (D-A500-03 decision gate).

Evidence: D-A500-01 D-A500-03 GAP-A-500-DF-243020 GAP-A-500-ID-5cb3b7 FEED-PE-REP-024

A500-02 · Design concept — dose-controlled cavitation loop

Adopted — loop is the process

Reasoning: The equipment arrangement must match the process concept: a recirculation loop with dose accounting, not a single-pass flow cell.

Observation: D-A500-03 adopts REP-024 §11: US-501 is a dose-controlled cavitation loop T-501 / P-501 / US-501 / HX-501 with N ≥ 3 turnovers, inter-pass cooling and forward transfer gated on dose accounting or an online proxy. The PFD-0500 recirculation line 507, the vendor-specified backpressure and flow, the start-up / maintenance bypass and the 30–40 °C outlet control to A-600 are approved (D-A500-02); T-501 is the surge / hold at the batch-to-continuous boundary (DBM V2.0 §3.4). The model shows the loop with three cells and the cooler in line.

Required action: Carry the loop concept into the P&ID (line 507 as a process line, dose accounting instrumentation in LST-007, transfer permissive) and derive the 30–40 °C limit from a measured thermal / sonochemical stability test instead of the unstated PFD origin.

Evidence: D-A500-03 D-A500-02 CF-cavitationBackpres-US-501 FEED-PE-PFD-001 FEED-PE-DBM-001

A500-03 · Solids concentration and commercial design basis

PFD basis approved — MDL-001 basis withdrawn

Reasoning: Loop flow, cell count and cooler duty scale with the solids concentration and the GO rate.

Observation: Approved: T-501 agitated at 1–2 wt% TLGO with no water or DMF added (PFD-0500 General Note 1, D-A500-02); REP-024 Lever 2 (run at the highest concentration the TFF delivers) is carried as the optimisation direction. Withdrawn: the MDL-001 basis of 64.98 / 103.97 kg/h GO solids and 5 kW per 10 kg pilot, which predates DBM V2.0 (D-A500-01). The Narrative (PRD-001 §4.10) still carries a DMF line.

Required action: MoC on FEED-PE-PRD-001 §4.10 and the capacity table: exclude DMF and state one solids-concentration basis shared by A-400 / A-500 / A-600; re-derive the A-500 rates from DBM V2.0 Table 3.3-1.

Evidence: D-A500-02 D-A500-01 CF-solidsConcentratio-T-501 GAP-A-500-DF-8ea93e GAP-A-500-ID-3e54f2 GAP-A-500-ID-7dc3eb FEED-PE-PRD-001

A500-04 · Materials and sonotrode erosion

Ruled — elemental limit to set

Reasoning: Wetted materials and sonotrode wear set the product-metal budget of the final rGO.

Observation: D-A500-MOC applies MAT-002: PVDF process-wetted envelope, or 316L electropolished where the product-metal budget allows, for T-501 / P-501 / HX-501 / E-501; MDL-001 (HDPE / XLPE tank, 316L pump and tubes) superseded. D-A500-03 approves the sonotrode as Ti Grade 5 (MAT-002 M-035) with a defined tip-replacement interval; REP-024's erosion / Ti-pickup bound is carried in the note. US-501 itself has an MDL-001 material only and stays candidate until MAT-002 covers it.

Required action: Set Ti / Fe / Cr limits in FEED-QA-SPC-001 and a tip-replacement schedule from measured sonotrode mass loss (REP-024 §12); extend MAT-002 to the US-501 cell body.

Evidence: D-A500-MOC D-A500-03 CF-materialOfConstruc-T-501 CF-materialOfConstruc-HX-501 GAP-A-500-DF-995546 GAP-A-500-DF-968713 FEED-PE-MAT-002

A500-05 · Register and model reconciliation

Register reconciliation required

Reasoning: Every equipment item in the area must have one identity in the register, the model and the data layer.

Observation: Three LST-001 tags (T-501, P-501, US-501) are bound to model geometry. The loop cooler is HX-501 in LST-001, PFD-0500, MAT-002 M-036 and MDL-001 but E-501 in the model (equipment 66) — same equipment, two tags. The model also owns P-502 (accepted-product transfer), BL-A500 and fire point FE-501, none on LST-001 / PFD-0500, and five interface records (CIP supply / return, sample, relief, drain) without destinations. LST-001 lists one US-501 flow cell; the model shows three cells.

Required action: Re-tag model equipment 66 to HX-501 or add E-501 to LST-001 by MoC; add P-502 and BL-A500 to PFD-0500 / LST-001; assign destinations to the five interfaces; record the cell count as open until the REP-024 program fixes N.

Evidence: GAP-A-500-ID-41e47c GAP-A-500-ID-6708fa GAP-A-500-ID-5cb3b7 HX-501 E-501 P-502 BL-A500 IF-A500-RELIEF

A500-06 · Enclosure, safeguards and operator access

Intent approved — detail on P&ID / HAZOP

Reasoning: A cavitation unit needs acoustic and vibration containment, a maintenance bypass and interlocked enclosure access.

Observation: D-A500-03 approves the safeguard intent: acoustic shielding, vibration isolation, maintenance bypass and enclosure interlocks as drawn (REP-024). The model places fire point FE-501 at the operator approach. Relief (IF-A500-RELIEF) and drain destinations, and the CIP supply / return interfaces, are not yet assigned; LST-007 carries only conceptual loop entries for A-500.

Required action: Define the enclosure interlocks, relief case and CIP chemistry on the P&ID; confirm the operator approach and fire-point position in the plant-wide access review; complete the A-500 loops in LST-007 once the dose instrumentation is chosen.

Evidence: D-A500-03 IF-A500-CIP-SUPPLY IF-A500-CIP-RETURN IF-A500-SAMPLE IF-A500-DRAIN FEED-PE-LST-007
