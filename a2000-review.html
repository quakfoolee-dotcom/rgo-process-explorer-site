Findings

A2000-01 · Feed and product basis

No basis approved — follows the A-1000 case

Reasoning: An RO plant is sized on its feed flow and quality and on the permeate demand; neither is fixed until the A-1000 discharge route and acid-recovery case are decided.

Observation: D-A2000-01 withdraws the RO-2001 feed basis and approves only the design statement that A-2000 sizing follows the acid-recovery decision (REP-039) and the A-1000 discharge route: the process-water demand build-up gives 8–18 m³/h net permeate (REP-008) against 17–120 m³/h of A-1000 effluent by case (REP-038). PFD-2000 has no stream table. The model's T-2001 1,000 m³, T-2002 140 m³ and T-2003 95 m³ are screening allowances, and the three RO trains A / B / C with P-2002 A / B / C exist only in the model — LST-001 and PFD-2000 list one RO-2001 and one P-2002.

Required action: Decide the A-1000 case, then issue the first A-2000 stream table on DBM V2.0 with the recovery, permeate quality and concentrate route; reconcile the three-train configuration with LST-001 / PFD-2000.

Evidence: D-A2000-01 GAP-A-2000-DF-4ca663 GAP-A-2000-ID-0c159a GAP-A-2000-ID-d8f929 RO-2001 T-2001 FEED-PE-REP-038 FEED-PE-REP-039

A2000-02 · RO feed condition — oxidiser, scaling and the NaOH question

Process constraint approved

Reasoning: The RO membrane tolerates neither residual oxidiser nor a feed beyond its sulphate scaling limit; the upstream chemistry decides whether RO is workable at all.

Observation: D-A2000-02 approves REP-038 as the process constraint: RO feed must be oxidiser-free and within BaSO₄ / CaSO₄ scaling limits; lime-stage gypsum saturation (≈ 1,480 mg/L) governs recovery; no NaOH upstream, because Na₂SO₄ at 61 g/L makes RO unworkable — which ties this area to the A-1000 flowsheet decision. P-2002 is approved as a 12–16 bar high-pressure pump (MAT-002 service register) as a screening basis. The model provides carbon filtration CF-2001 for oxidant / organic reduction and antiscalant dosing T-2004 / P-2004 ahead of the trains.

Required action: Carry the oxidiser-free and scaling constraints into the A-1000 MoC (lime-first, no NaOH) and into the RO design basis; confirm the antiscalant and recovery against the gypsum limit once the feed is fixed.

Evidence: D-A2000-02 GAP-A-2000-DF-188a44 RO-2001 P-2002 CF-2001 T-2004 FEED-PE-REP-038

A2000-03 · Pretreatment and backwash

Configuration as modelled — duties open

Reasoning: The activated-carbon filter CF-2001 is the only pretreatment left (D-A2000-03: clean condensate); its backwash is taken from the permeate tank T-2002 and returns to A-1000 T-1006, and must be sized with it.

Observation: The multimedia, greensand and ultrafiltration pretreatment (MMF-2001, GF-2001, UF-2001) and its buffer tank, pumps and backwash collection were retired by D-A2000-03 and removed from the model. CF-2001 filters the T-2001 condensate and is backwashed with RO permeate from T-2002 through P-2005; the backwash waste joins the RO reject to A-1000 T-1006. The control station CP-2000 remains model-only and is not on LST-001; no filtration duty is approved.

Required action: Size CF-2001 and its permeate backwash on the A-2000 stream table; register CP-2000 or record it by decision; state the backwash return in the A-1000 balance.

Evidence: GAP-A-2000-ID-d0aead CF-2001 CP-2000

A2000-04 · Cleaning-in-place and spent CIP

Configuration as modelled — chemistry open

Reasoning: Sequential acid / alkaline cleaning liquor is a segregated waste and must not reach the RO feed or the A-1000 lime stage uncontrolled.

Observation: The model provides the register CIP skid CIP-2001 with recirculation pump P-CIP2001, a segregated spent-CIP hold TK-CIP2001-W (5 m³ allowance) with transfer pump P-CIP2001-W, and the antiscalant day tank T-2004 / P-2004. The CIP chemistry, frequency and spent-liquor destination are not defined; the spent-CIP tank and the two CIP pumps (P-CIP2001, P-CIP2001-W) exist only as model geometry without semantic records.

Required action: Define the CIP chemistry and cycle with the membrane selection; assign the spent-CIP destination (A-1000 stage or off-site); register the model-only tags.

Evidence: CIP-2001 TK-CIP2001-W T-2004 P-2004 GAP-A-2000-ID-d0aead MODEL

A2000-05 · Materials of construction

Ruled — RO-specific wording to refine

Reasoning: Pretreated, low-solids water at up to 16 bar sets pressure-vessel and pump materials; the membranes themselves are a selection, not a material ruling.

Observation: D-A2000-MOC applies MAT-002 to fourteen items with the RO service row — TFC polyamide RO elements in FRP pressure vessels, with the matching tank, pump, filter-housing and CIP selections; MDL-001 (HDPE / XLPE tanks, 316L pumps and housings, PVDF UF modules) superseded. The single MAT-002 row covers the whole area, so the tank and pump wording is generic and will need item-level refinement at datasheet stage.

Required action: Refine MAT-002 per item (tanks, pumps, filter housings, UF modules) once the stream table exists; keep the TFC / FRP RO selection.

Evidence: D-A2000-MOC CF-materialOfConstruc-P-2002 CF-materialOfConstruc-T-2001 FEED-PE-MAT-002

A2000-06 · A-2000 on clean sources only (D-A2000-03): condensate polishing and municipal make-up; the A-1000 effluent is not reclaimed; 15 tags retired

Basis approved; datasheets issued as candidates; demand held on A-400 (H-01)

Reasoning: D-A2000-01 left A-2000 unsized until the acid route and the A-1000 discharge were settled (A2000-01); with Route 2 + 6 both are now known, and the effluent turned out to be the wrong source.

Observation: On the Case 4 volumes the A-300 phase 2 HCl dominates the A-1000 effluent: ≈ 5.9 g/L chloride and ≈ 12 g/L TDS at the M3 estimate, ≈ 29 g/L chloride and ≈ 48 g/L TDS at the PFD dose — lime does not remove chloride. Reclaiming it would need brackish-to-seawater RO and 4–26 t/d of soda-ash softening, against ≈ 380 m³/d of municipal water (≈ $0.15–0.3 M/y). D-A2000-03 on bases A2-1–A2-8 approved by QFL 2026-09-27 (FEED-PE-CAL-036, FEED-PE-DAT-132…139, register V9.0) puts A-2000 on clean sources only: the A-1100 condensate (2.6 m³/h, 3.0 at the 150 t/d rating) is pH-trimmed in T-2001 (re-purposed, 15 m³), passed through carbon (CF-2001) and polished on RO-2001A/B (1 + 1, 85 %, 10 bar, P-2002A/B) to ≈ 2.2 m³/h; ≈ 15.8 m³/h of municipal water enters through CF-2002 (IF-A2000-CITY); T-2002 (100 m³, 4 h) and P-2005A/B (20 m³/h × 35 m) serve the 18 m³/h demand. The RO reject (≈ 0.5 m³/h) goes to T-1006 and the A-1000 effluent to sewer. The media / greensand / UF train, the backwash and brine handling and the third RO train — 15 tags — are retired; the CAL-036 feed basis, design concept and 10 bar feed pressure replace the D-A2000-01 / -02 statements; 12 missing-value gaps close. W1 (reclaim) stays a noted option, gated on M3 and a water-reuse requirement.

Required action: Settle the A-400 demand (hold H-01) and scale T-2002 / P-2005 / the municipal allowance to it; obtain the municipal supply data (pressure, hardness, connection); confirm the condensate quality with the concentrator vendor (M5) and on reconcentrated M1 filtrate (M2); raise the PFD-2000 MoC and LST-001 entries; remove the retired tags from the model.
