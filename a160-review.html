Findings

A160-01 · Dilution basis — Option B now, Option C later

Design concept approved — sizing withdrawn

Reasoning: The fixing-water quantity decides tank size, cooling, settle / decant practice and the wastewater load; the PFD figure is a heat sink, not a process requirement.

Observation: D-A160-02 approves the REP-042 concept — Option B now, designed so that its filter and rinse become Option C when REP-039 M4 confirms GIC filterability — and D-A160-01 withdraws the MEB-004 V5 sizing on 50 kg/h graphite and 170 kg water per kg graphite (T-161 49.2 m³; REP-042 shows ≈ 2 × 110 m³ HDPE as drawn and recommends 10–15 t per batch, about 20–30 kg/kg), the 28 kW dilution cooling (REP-042: 6.2 GJ per batch dilution heat), the settle / decant hours (pilot 3 × 3.5 h do not transfer — Stokes settling scales with depth; decant is dilution washing), the T-162 wash water and the D-164 duty. Feed now arrives at 80 °C by hot transfer (D-A140-02); no jacket on a 110 m³ HDPE tank — protect the addition point against local boiling.

Required action: Decide the A-160 dilution basis (Option B with the Option C path), re-issue FEED-PE-MEB-004 (V6) on DBM V2.0, then fix T-161 / T-162 size and cooling, and confirm water inlet ≤ 28 °C keeps T-161 below 50 °C.

A160-02 · Filter press and cake basis

Approved — GREEN, cake-volume-limited

Reasoning: The press cycle and cake moisture set the dryer duty and the tray handling rate.

Observation: D-A160-02 approves F-161 as correctly sized and cake-volume-limited (REP-042 F-10, GREEN): 3 wash / press cycles, 6 h cycle, cake 0.57 m³ per batch, displacement wash ≈ 2 m³; cake moisture 50 wt% (MEB-004) — REP-042 states ≈ 80 wt% solids after press, a difference carried to the follow-up. F-161 is PP plates and cloth with protected frame (MAT-002). Wet-cake tray handling TR-164 and the register pumps P-164 (slurry to press) and P-165 (filtrate wastewater) have model geometry; P-162 … P-165 have no semantic record.

Required action: Reconcile the 50 wt% moisture (MEB-004) with the ≈ 80 wt% solids (REP-042) in MEB-004 V6; create semantic records for P-162 … P-165.

Evidence: D-A160-02 F-161 TR-164 P-164 P-165 GAP-A-160-ID-2d969e FEED-PE-REP-042 FEED-PE-MEB-004

A160-03 · Drying — vacuum tray dryer as modelled, Option E open

Targets approved — dryer type follows Option E

Reasoning: The dryer type and duty follow the cake solids and the REP-042 Option E alternative.

Observation: Approved (D-A160-02, MEB-004): 60 °C drying temperature target and 2 wt% residual moisture; D-164 316L electropolished (MAT-002). The 47.3 kW duty on 50 kg/h is withdrawn and REP-042 Option E changes the dryer. The model carries the vacuum tray dryer with its vacuum / condensate package VP-164 / KO-164, dry tray unloading H-164 and the dry Pre-G distribution EL-164 / SC-164 (4,586 parts) — none of these auxiliaries is on LST-001, and the D-164-THERMAL subassembly is provisional.

Required action: Decide Option E with the dilution basis; then size D-164 (or its replacement) on MEB-004 V6 and register VP-164 / KO-164, H-164, TR-164 and EL-164 / SC-164 or record them by decision.

Evidence: D-A160-02 D-A160-01 D-164 VP-164-KO-164 EL-164-SC-164 H-164 D-164-THERMAL GAP-A-160-ID-295995 FEED-PE-REP-042

A160-04 · Materials of construction

Ruled — six items not yet covered

Reasoning: Dilute sulphuric / persulfate liquor at up to 80 °C sets the tank and pump materials; the press and dryer see wet and dry Pre-G.

Observation: D-A160-MOC applies MAT-002: T-161 dual-laminate FRP (PVDF / ECTFE liner + VE-novolac) to ASME RTP-1; F-161 PP plates and cloth; D-164 316L electropolished; MDL-001 (glass-lined CS, PP plates) superseded. MAT-002 does not cover T-162, T-163 and the four pumps P-162 … P-165, which keep an MDL-001 material only. REP-042 speaks of 110 m³ HDPE tanks as drawn — the tank material and the dilution option are linked.

Required action: Extend MAT-002 to T-162, T-163 and P-162 … P-165 once the dilution option fixes tank size and temperature.

Evidence: D-A160-MOC CF-materialOfConstruc-T-161 CF-materialOfConstruc-F-161 GAP-A-160-DF-5c3794 FEED-PE-MAT-002

A160-05 · Register and model reconciliation, containment and interfaces

Register reconciliation required

Reasoning: Every equipment item in the area must have one identity in the register, the model and the data layer; wastewater and decant interfaces need destinations.

Observation: Five LST-001 tags (T-161, T-162, F-161, D-164, T-163) are bound to model geometry; the four register pumps have geometry but no semantic record; the model additionally owns TR-164, H-164, EL-164 / SC-164 and VP-164 / KO-164 without register identities, four bunds (16.4 / 16.4 / 10.4 / 11.5 m³ retention), two emergency stations and fire point FE-161. Three interfaces — fixing wastewater WW161, filtrate wastewater WW163 and decant DEC161 — have no destination in A-1000.

Required action: Register the model-only auxiliaries or record them by decision; create the pump records; assign the WW161 / WW163 / DEC161 destinations with the A-1000 discharge decision (D-A1000-01); verify bund retention for the hot-transfer case.
