Findings

A1000-01 · Discharge route and acid recovery — the governing decision

Design lever identified — no flow approved

Reasoning: The wastewater flow, its acid load and therefore every reactor, reagent and sludge quantity depend on whether spent acid is recovered before A-1000.

Observation: D-A1000-01 approves the REP-038 first-principles load as the design statement — 0.64 mol/L H₂SO₄ + 0.23 mol/L HCl (179 t/d H₂SO₄, 24 t/d HCl, 129 kmol/d H₃PO₄) with Mn / K / P: the feed is dilute acid, not wastewater — and REP-039's acid recovery (150–200 t/d spent-acid reconcentration, 1.5–4.4 MW at 150–200 °C) as the governing design lever. No flow is approved: 2,850 m³/d as drawn, 400–600 m³/d with acid recovery, or the DBM 30–75 m³/d corrected by MOC-001 to a range by case. No A-1000 stream table exists; the model volumes (T-1006 200 m³, R-1001 35 m³, R-1002 65 m³, R-1003 / R-1004 45 m³) are screening allowances.

Required action: Decide the A-1000 discharge route and the spent-acid recovery case (REP-039; MOC-001 Part D.1) before any A-1000 / A-2000 sizing; then issue the first A-1000 / A-2000 stream tables on DBM V2.0 with the wastewater range by case (REP-038).

Evidence: D-A1000-01 GAP-A-1000-DF-329a40 GAP-A-1000-ID-e02f06 T-1006 FEED-PE-REP-038 FEED-PE-REP-039

A1000-02 · Treatment chemistry — NaOH stage not viable, lime-first flowsheet

Intent adopted — MoC on PFD-1000 required

Reasoning: At 0.64 mol/L the drawn rapid NaOH neutralisation would consume ≈ 166 t/d NaOH and leave sulphate soluble, making downstream RO unworkable.

Observation: D-A1000-02 adopts REP-038: the NaOH "rapid" stage to pH 3–4 is not viable (≈ 166 t/d NaOH, ≈ $22 M/y; sulphate stays soluble; Na₂SO₄ at 61 g/L makes RO unworkable) — lime-first flowsheet adopted as intent, with R-1001's duty to be redefined; the lime stage to pH 8.5–10.5 at ≈ 149 t/d Ca(OH)₂ producing ≈ 314 t/d gypsum + 20 t/d Ca₃(PO₄)₂ + 5 t/d Mn sludge (Case 0) as the chemistry basis; BaCl₂ polishing (R-1003) conditional on the local sewer sulphate limit vs gypsum saturation (≈ 1,480 mg/L). The ≈ 2 MW neutralisation heat and the ≈ 340 t/d sludge rate are withdrawn as case-dependent. The model still carries the NaOH tank T-1001 and dosing pump P-1004 as drawn.

Required action: Raise the MoC on FEED-PFD-1000: remove the NaOH rapid-neutralisation stage or re-purpose R-1001 (lime / limestone-then-lime high-density sludge); restate T-1001 / T-1002 and the dosing pumps; fix the sulphate limit with the receiving authority.

Evidence: D-A1000-02 GAP-A-1000-DF-21a95a GAP-A-1000-ID-e796a7 R-1001 R-1002 R-1003 T-1001 P-1004 FEED-PE-REP-038

A1000-03 · Sludge, decanter and centrate

Case-dependent — no rate approved

Reasoning: Gypsum-dominated sludge at hundreds of tonnes per day decides the decanter, the bin and the truck movements; all follow the acid-recovery case.

Observation: D-A1000-02 withdrew the DC-1001 feed basis (≈ 340 t/d sludge in Case 0). The model provides the decanter with feed pump P-1009 and a conditional bypass pump P-1010, a covered sludge bin BIN-1001 whose classification is pending, an 8 m³ centrate receiver T-1008 with transfer / recycle pump P-1012, and the Pre-G gravity-decant sump T-1007 / P-1011 — the last five are model-only tags without register identity.

Required action: Size DC-1001, BIN-1001 and the sludge handling on the chosen case; classify the sludge (gypsum with Mn / P) for disposal; register the model-only tags or record them by decision.

Evidence: D-A1000-02 GAP-A-1000-ID-79e3fe DC-1001 BIN-1001 T-1008 T-1007 P-1011 P-1012

A1000-04 · Materials of construction

Ruled — M-1001 and T-1005 not yet covered

Reasoning: Dilute mixed acid, lime slurry, BaCl₂, HCl and gypsum slurry each set their own wetted materials.

Observation: D-A1000-MOC applies MAT-002 to sixteen items: dual-laminate FRP / HDPE / rubber-lined carbon steel for the acid-side tanks and reactors (T-1006, R-1001 … R-1004), HDPE or rubber-lined carbon steel for lime storage (T-1002), and the matching pump and centrifuge selections; MDL-001 (XLPE / HDPE, rubber-lined CS) superseded. M-1001 and T-1005 (floc aid) keep an MDL-001 material only.

Required action: Extend MAT-002 to M-1001 and T-1005; issue datasheets once the flowsheet (A1000-02) and the case (A1000-01) are fixed.

Evidence: D-A1000-MOC CF-materialOfConstruc-T-1006 CF-materialOfConstruc-T-1002 GAP-A-1000-DF-f06416 FEED-PE-MAT-002

A1000-05 · Containment, access and inbound interfaces

Model provision — retention unverified

Reasoning: A reagent farm with NaOH, lime, BaCl₂, HCl and 200 m³ of acidic equalization needs retention, access and emergency provision sized to the real inventories.

Observation: The model owns twelve bunds (from 4.6 m³ under floc-aid storage to 265 m³ under the equalization tank — all retention figures scale from screening volumes), the access / containment structure PL-1001, control station CP-1001, three fire points and seven emergency stations, none on LST-001. The inbound streams from A-300 (acidic centrate) and A-400 (wash water) are model interfaces without a stated flow; the A-160 wastewater and decant interfaces have no A-1000 destination.

Required action: Re-derive retention from the case-based inventories; assign the inbound interface flows in the stream tables; register bunds, stations, fire points and PL-1001 / CP-1001 or record them by decision.

Evidence: IF-A300-WASTE IF-A400-WASTE PL-1001 CP-1001 MODEL
