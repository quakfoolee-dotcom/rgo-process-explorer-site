A1000-02 · Treatment chemistry — NaOH stage not viable, lime-first flowsheet

Intent adopted — MoC on PFD-1000 required

Reasoning: At 0.64 mol/L the drawn rapid NaOH neutralisation would consume ≈ 166 t/d NaOH and leave sulphate soluble, making downstream RO unworkable.

Observation: D-A1000-02 adopts REP-038: the NaOH "rapid" stage to pH 3–4 is not viable (≈ 166 t/d NaOH, ≈ $22 M/y; sulphate stays soluble; Na₂SO₄ at 61 g/L makes RO unworkable) — lime-first flowsheet adopted as intent, with R-1001's duty to be redefined; the lime stage to pH 8.5–10.5 at ≈ 149 t/d Ca(OH)₂ producing ≈ 314 t/d gypsum + 20 t/d Ca₃(PO₄)₂ + 5 t/d Mn sludge (Case 0) as the chemistry basis; BaCl₂ polishing (R-1003) conditional on the local sewer sulphate limit vs gypsum saturation (≈ 1,480 mg/L). The ≈ 2 MW neutralisation heat and the ≈ 340 t/d sludge rate are withdrawn as case-dependent. The model still carries the NaOH tank T-1001 and dosing pump P-1004 as drawn.

Required action: Raise the MoC on FEED-PFD-1000: remove the NaOH rapid-neutralisation stage or re-purpose R-1001 (lime / limestone-then-lime high-density sludge); restate T-1001 / T-1002 and the dosing pumps; fix the sulphate limit with the receiving authority.
