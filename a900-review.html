Findings

A900-01 · Two trains, four shared tags — identity holds

Identity holds active — no register values applied

Reasoning: A value approved for the register equipment must not be written into a model record that denotes different equipment, even when the tag is the same.

Observation: The register (PFD-0900 / LST-001) is a densification train — T-901 → H-901 → C-901 → RC-901 roll compactor → M-901 mill → SC-901 screener → PK-901 cartridge filler → W-901 → SE-901, with DC-901 / FN-901 area extraction. The model (V162 / V163) is a technology-neutral pelletization line — TR-901 → H-901 → F-901 → PG-901 → SC-901 cooling / classification → PK-901 collection package, with RB-901, DC-901 local capture and CP-901 — built without the FEED-PFD-0900 package. Four tags coincide with different meanings; QFL placed H-901, SC-901, PK-901 and DC-901 on identity hold on 2026-09-20, so their six approved rows (materials, inerting basis, product specification) are listed as held and not applied. Seven register items (T-901, C-901, RC-901, M-901, W-901, SE-901, FN-901) have no model record; five model items (TR-901, F-901, PG-901, RB-901, CP-901) have no register identity.

Required action: Reconcile the two trains by MoC on FEED-PFD-0900 — either adopt the pelletization line and retire the densification tags, or model the register train — then lift or convert the holds; until then the model A-900 is a layout placeholder.

Evidence: H-901 SC-901 PK-901 DC-901 GAP-A-900-ID-100582 GAP-A-900-ID-6060eb GAP-A-900-ID-1f4b5d FEED-PE-LST-001

A900-02 · Product acceptance criterion and mechanism

NOT DEFINED — gating item (REP-023 RED)

Reasoning: The compaction target must state which density it means and whether the storage mechanism tolerates the loss of micropore volume.

Observation: D-A900-01 records the acceptance criterion as NOT DEFINED: "1.6 g/mL pellets" does not say whether it is ribbon envelope, granule or packed-cartridge density, and a 1.6 g/mL envelope on a 2.1 g/cm³ carbon leaves ≈ 0.15 cm³/g pore volume — incompatible with a physisorption carbon (0.5–1.0 cm³/g). RC-901 is approved as volume-limited (0.2–1.5 m³/h at 30–200 kg/m³ bulk density: bulk density governs, not mass rate). The model's PG-901 is explicitly a "technology HOLD" envelope.

Required action: Close REP-023's five design-basis items — density definition, product-rate basis, potassium form and ignition sensitivity, A-800 discharge cooling responsibility, inerting extent — then select the densification technology and size the train.

Evidence: D-A900-01 GAP-A-900-DF-fc2b25 GAP-A-900-ID-9dd744 PG-901 FEED-PE-REP-023

A900-03 · Product rate basis

Basis withdrawn — three rates on record

Reasoning: Every item in the area is sized on the packed-product rate, which must be single-valued on the DBM V2.0 basis.

Observation: Three rates are on record: 28 / 26 kg/h (Narrative capacity table), 44.91 / 42.67 kg/h (MEB V2.0 / REP-019 after 0.95 densification yield) and the DBM V2.0 packed product 625 / 812 kg/d nominal / design (≈ 26 / 34 kg/h). D-A900-01 withdrew the RC-901 feed rate; D-A900-02 approves the DBM V2.0 packed-product rate as the T-901 feed basis. The A-800 → A-900 product handoff is the interface IF-A800-PRODUCT.

Required action: Restate the A-900 rate once on DBM V2.0 in the Narrative and MEB; carry the 0.95 densification yield and the reject stream explicitly.

Evidence: D-A900-01 D-A900-02 GAP-A-900-ID-2f622c IF-A800-PRODUCT FEED-PE-DBM-001 FEED-PE-REP-019

A900-04 · Reactive hazard, inerting and dust extraction

Intent approved — extent unresolved

Reasoning: Until the residual potassium form in the product is known, the compaction and milling enclosures are water-reactive combustible-dust service.

Observation: D-A900-01 approves the REP-023 safeguard statement for RC-901 / M-901: residual potassium form (metallic, K₂O / KOH or bound) unresolved, ignition-sensitive temperature unmeasured, A-800 discharge temperature and cooling responsibility to be assigned. D-A900-02 adopts the PFD-0900 inerting reading — T-901 argon-blanketed, AR902 purge to the compaction enclosure "as required", AR903 to filling, enclosed handling at slight negative pressure via DC-901 / FN-901. The model reserves the three argon takeoffs as positively blinded branches (IF-A900-AR901 … 903) and models DC-901 as a local capture on PG-901 without FN-901.

Required action: Resolve the potassium form (REP-022 / REP-023) and the ignition sensitivity, then fix the inerting extent (which enclosures, which purge) and the extraction philosophy (local vs area) before the dust-hazard assessment; connect the argon branches when the train is settled.

Evidence: D-A900-01 D-A900-02 IF-A900-AR901 IF-A900-AR902 IF-A900-AR903 DC-901 FEED-PE-REP-023

A900-05 · Product release and filling

Specification draft — held on PK-901

Reasoning: Filling and release need the product specification and the hold point that gates dispatch.

Observation: D-A900-02 approves product release per FEED-QA-SPC-001 (rGO-BCK specification, stream 804, draft V0.1) with hold point HP-1, and a checkweigher verification before sealing (W-901). Both are recorded against the register's cartridge filler PK-901; the model's PK-901 is a collection / packaging envelope, so the rows are held. W-901 and SE-901 have no model record; the model's accepted / reject / dust boundaries are the interfaces IF-A900-PRODUCT / REJECT / DUST.

Required action: Issue FEED-QA-SPC-001 beyond draft with the package format decided; then map release, checkweigh and seal functions onto whichever train is adopted and lift the PK-901 hold.

Evidence: D-A900-02 PK-901 W-901 SE-901 IF-A900-PRODUCT IF-A900-REJECT FEED-QA-SPC-001

A900-06 · Materials of construction

Ruled for the register train — held on shared tags

Reasoning: Product-contact surfaces for a reactive combustible carbon powder must be grounded, electropolished and hard-faced at wear points.

Observation: D-A900-MOC applies MAT-002 to the eleven register items: 316L electropolished, grounded and bonded product-contact surfaces with hard-facing (M-055) and conductive grounded 316L or conductive PTFE-lined ducting for DC-901 / FN-901 (M-056); MDL-001 304L superseded. Seven of the eleven rulings sit on register-only items without a model record; the four on shared tags are held. The model-only items TR-901, F-901, PG-901, RB-901 and CP-901 have no material at all.

Required action: Once the train is reconciled, extend MAT-002 to the adopted model items and apply the rulings; keep the M-055 / M-056 basis for whichever equipment survives.

Evidence: D-A900-MOC CF-materialOfConstruc-RC-901 CF-materialOfConstruc-DC-901 TR-901 F-901 RB-901 FEED-PE-MAT-002

A900-07 · Layout, supports and access

Provisional geometry — vendor loads unverified

Reasoning: A placeholder line still needs a plausible footprint, supports and access so that the plant-wide reviews remain meaningful.

Observation: V162 relocated the line beside the cooled PY-801 outlet and removed the former long transfer route and its three walkway conflicts; V163 added floor-connected skids, a feeder frame, a dust-filter frame, a reject tray and a control-cabinet plinth, all provisional (vendor loads, member sizing, anchors and vibration isolation unverified). The model places fire point FE-901 at the future filling / dispatch approach; no emergency station is assigned to A-900. The packaged-product route to dispatch is not modelled.

Required action: Keep the footprint as the reserved envelope; qualify supports and access once a technology is selected; add an emergency station and the dispatch route to the plant-wide access and fire-safety reviews.
