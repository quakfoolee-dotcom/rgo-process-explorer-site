Findings

A700-01 · Furnace configuration — three modules by closure, one in the model

Design basis approved — datasheet, PFD and model to follow

Reasoning: The furnace count, retort size and installed duty are the area's governing design statement; every downstream item follows them.

Observation: D-A700-01 approves the REP-018 design closure as the FEED design-basis statement: three identical indirectly electrically heated screw-furnace modules PY-701A/B/C, 0.90 m ID × 12 m heated retort (15 m overall), 180 kW per module in six zones, DN200 off-gas at 650 m³/h and 1,100 °C per module, hot zone UNS N06230 with Kanthal APMT as qualified alternative. DAT-004, PFD-0700, LST-001 and the model all show a single PY-701 (equipment 83); the single-unit statements are superseded, not deleted.

Required action: Update FEED-PE-DAT-004 (V1.1) to the three-module basis and complete its 55 blank items; add PY-701B/C geometry and records to the model and LST-001; raise the MoC on MAT-002 M-042 / M-045 for the N06230 selection or record the reason to keep Alloy 602 CA.

Evidence: D-A700-01 GAP-A-700-ID-f36845 GAP-A-700-ID-35239f FEED-PE-REP-018 FEED-PE-DAT-004

A700-02 · Feed-rate basis

Basis withdrawn — four rates on record

Reasoning: Furnace, feeder and cooler duties scale with the dried-TLGO rate, which must be single-valued on the DBM V2.0 basis.

Observation: Four rates are on record: 22.5 kg/h (MEB-009 V10, on 50 kg/h graphite), 44.1 kg/h (DAT-004), 3 × 46 kg/h (REP-018) and the DBM V2.0 design rate of 74 kg/h dried TLGO (57 kg/h nominal). D-A700-04 withdrew the feed rate, product rate and the MEB-009 screening duties for PY-701, C-701, E-701 and E-702; the MEB-009 vs DAT-004 conflict stays open until MEB-009 is re-issued.

Required action: Re-issue FEED-PE-MEB-009 (V11) on DBM V2.0 (57 / 74 kg/h dried TLGO), restate the module rates and duties, then derive E-701 / E-702 duties and the C-701 feeder rating.

Evidence: D-A700-04 CF-feedRateKgh-PY-701 GAP-A-700-CF-9f8fa8 FEED-PE-MEB-009 FEED-PE-DBM-001

A700-03 · Operating envelope and design inconsistencies

Approved — two RED inconsistencies to resolve

Reasoning: Temperature, residence, ramp, atmosphere and pressure define the reduction and the hazardous-area basis.

Observation: Approved (D-A700-02): 1,100 °C reaction temperature and 4 h active-zone residence (DAT-004, MEB-009, REP-018 / 021 agree), ≤ 10 ppm O₂ operating and pre-heat, −3 mbar(g) furnace pressure (MEB-009, consistent with the PSV-701 80 mbarg set / 100 mbarg MAWP), indirect heating with the process isolated from the elements, Zone 1 internal (H₂ service + combustible dust). The 5 °C/min ramp is recorded as the stated basis, but REP-021 shows it cannot fit within the 4 h residence — recorded as an open RED item together with the deoxygenation sequence.

Required action: Resolve the ramp / residence inconsistency and the deoxygenation basis in DAT-004 V1.1; fill the PY-701 design pressure (blank in DAT-004) consistent with the 100 mbarg MAWP used by DAT-007.

Evidence: D-A700-02 GAP-A-700-ID-b1cedb FEED-PE-DAT-004 FEED-PE-REP-021 FEED-PE-MEB-009

A700-04 · Off-gas basis, vent segregation and relief

Segregation required — S2 header

Reasoning: CO-in-argon furnace gas is a dry inert combustible stream; it must never share a header with wet oxidising vents or with the A-800 hydrogen / potassium stream.

Observation: D-A700-02 adopts REP-041: CO 4–8 Nm³/h steady (× 3–5 in the burst), 8–10 vol % in argon, water dew point 60–70 °C — segregated as system S2 from the wet S1 vents and from the A-800 S3 stream; the PFD-3000 common header is superseded as intent. PSV-701 (DAT-007) is approved at 80 mbarg set, 100 mbarg MAWP, ASME VIII + API 526, SS316L / Inconel 625 hot, safe atmospheric vent with a rupture disc in series recommended; PSV-701 has no model geometry. KO-701 (DAT-006): 200 °C / 100 mbarg / full vacuum, ≥ 90 % fines removal, ≤ 10 mbar, conical 60° head.

Required action: Confirm the three-system segregation (S1 / S2 / S3) on FEED-PFD-3000 and in HAZOP; size E-701 for the 60–70 °C dew point and fines; add PSV-701 to the model at its mounting location (hot-side body temperature governs its material) and complete the DAT-007 relieving-capacity items.

Evidence: D-A700-02 D-A700-03 PSV-701 KO-701 IF-A700-RELIEF FEED-PE-REP-041 FEED-PE-DAT-007 FEED-PE-DAT-006

A700-05 · Product cooling and receiver

Datasheet values approved — scope question open

Reasoning: The product must leave the inert envelope cooled below its ignition-sensitive temperature into a contained receiver.

Observation: E-702 (DAT-005) approved: 60–80 °C solids outlet, 200 °C / +50 mbarg / full vacuum design, 1.5 mm corrosion allowance, 32 °C CWS / 40 °C CWR. DAT-005 itself asks the furnace vendor whether E-702 is essential or a safety margin against under-performance of the PY-701 internal cooling zone; its duty, cooling-water flows and residence time are blank. T-702 is ruled 316L (MAT-002). The T-701 cyclone named in the design notes is not on PFD-0700, LST-001 or in the model.

Required action: Decide the E-702 scope with the furnace vendor (essential vs margin), complete the DAT-005 duty and cooling-water items on the re-issued MEB-009, and rule the T-701 cyclone in or out of PFD-0700.

Evidence: D-A700-03 E-702 T-702 GAP-A-700-ID-d6c59a T-701 FEED-PE-DAT-005

A700-06 · Materials of construction

Ruled — hot-zone alloy to align by MoC

Reasoning: Carburising, reducing service at 1,100 °C with carbon-bearing solids is aggressive to stainless steels and to some nickel alloys (metal dusting window).

Observation: D-A700-MOC applies MAT-002: E-701 Alloy 602 CA or alonised Alloy 800H in the metal-dusting window; C-701 316L / 310S at the cool end and Alloy 601 / 602 CA at the hot transition; T-702 in 316L; FN-701 / PSV wetted metal in 316L or Alloy 601 / 602 CA where hot. D-A700-01 approves REP-018's hot-zone UNS N06230 for PY-701 over MAT-002 M-042 (Alloy 602 CA), and D-A700-03 the DAT-006 SS316L shell for KO-701; MDL-001 (Inconel 601 retort, 304L casings) is superseded.

Required action: Align MAT-002 M-042 / M-045 / M-047 with the N06230 selection and the KO-701 316L shell by MoC; confirm the carburisation life target (25,000 h screening) with the vendor.

Evidence: D-A700-MOC D-A700-01 CF-materialOfConstruc-PY-701 CF-materialOfConstruc-E-701 CF-materialOfConstruc-C-701 FEED-PE-MAT-002

A700-07 · Register and model reconciliation

Register reconciliation required

Reasoning: Every equipment item in the area must have one identity in the register, the model and the data layer.

Observation: Five LST-001 tags (C-701, PY-701, E-701, FN-701, T-702) are bound to model geometry. The model additionally owns the proposed contained feed chain TR-701 / LK-701 / H-701, the product cooler E-702, the separator KO-701, the instrument assembly AIT-701, the battery limit BL-700-UTIL, two fire points and one emergency station — none on LST-001. PSV-701 has a datasheet and approved values but no geometry; the T-701 cyclone has neither. Relief and condensate interfaces have no destinations.

Required action: Add TR-701, LK-701, H-701, E-702, KO-701, AIT-701 and PSV-701 to LST-001 and PFD-0700 (or record them as proposed by decision); model PSV-701; rule T-701; assign the relief and condensate destinations.

Evidence: GAP-A-700-ID-ee9ffc GAP-A-700-ID-d6c59a TR-701 LK-701 H-701 AIT-701 BL-700-UTIL IF-A700-CONDENSATE

A700-08 · Datasheet completeness

55 blank items across four unsigned datasheets

Reasoning: Unsigned datasheets with blank duty, flow, weight and rating items cannot support a vendor enquiry.

Observation: DAT-004 (furnace), DAT-005 (cooler), DAT-006 (separator) and DAT-007 (PSV) are controlled but unsigned; the data layer lists 55 blank items — among them the furnace zone duties and installed capacity, shell design temperature, drive ratings and operating weights; the cooler duty, cooling-water flows and residence time; the separator gas and solids flows; the PSV required and certified relieving capacities, ratings and facings. Most depend on the withdrawn feed-rate basis (A700-02).

Required action: Complete and sign DAT-004 … DAT-007 on the re-issued MEB-009 basis; the blank-item list in the evidence register is the work-list.
