Findings

A3000-01 · Vent routing philosophy — three segregated systems

Design intent approved — MoC on PFD-3000 required

Reasoning: H₂, O₂ and CO in one header, and potassium vapour into water, are the two failure modes the as-drawn single-header PFD-3000 creates; treatment must match the contaminant and the pressure regime of each source.

Observation: D-A3000-01 adopts REP-041 as the design intent: S1 — the wet caustic scrubber on wet vents only (a few hundred to ≈ 1,000 Nm³/h; A-300 quench O₂ ≈ 160 Nm³/h peak); S2 — PY-701 CO / CO₂ / H₂O in argon (4–8 Nm³/h CO steady, ×3–5 in the burst, 8–10 vol %) to a thermal treatment; S3 — PY-801 H₂ in argon (12–35 Nm³/h, 30–55 vol %) with 10–20 kg/h potassium vapour to a hot-end condenser with no water contact. PFD-3000 as drawn routes every vent to one knock-out drum and caustic scrubber; LST-001 lists only the seven wet-train tags. The model already carries the segregated branches AB-3701 and AB-3801 and the two dry packages, and its exhaust-group check confirms they are not accidentally connected. WorkSafeBC Part 5.71 requires separate exhaust where combining combustible sources creates a fire or explosion risk.

Required action: Raise the MoC on FEED-PFD-3000 for S1 / S2 / S3; add the S2 / S3 packages and the dry-vent packages to LST-001; confirm the segregation in HAZOP and against the actual source duties.

A3000-02 · S1 wet-vent source register and sizing basis

Basis approved — no source register, nothing sized

Reasoning: The scrubber, fan and stack are sized on the simultaneous peak of the wet sources; without a source register with compositions there is no duty.

Observation: D-A3000-01 approves the S1 basis — wet vents only, a few hundred to ≈ 1,000 Nm³/h — and records that A-600 is not an A-3000 load (its own F-601 / SC-601 in the DBM inert closed loop; 333,000 Nm³/h if it were air-swept). The model header HD-3100 collects the A-100 / A-140 / A-160 tank vents, A-200 AV201, A-300, A-500 and the A-600 tank vent as interface records without composition or flow. No source register with simultaneous peaks exists; the model's "candidate wet source register" is a placeholder, and the fan is "VFD proposed" without a duty.

Required action: Issue the S1 source register (composition, normal and peak flow, simultaneity, pressure limits of each connected vessel), then size V-3110, T-3140, ME-3150, FN-3160 and ST-3170 and qualify the stack height.

Evidence: D-A3000-01 FN-3160 HD-3100 IF-A300-VENT IF-A500-VENT IF-A600-TANK-VENT IF-A3000-AV201 IF-A3000-V-R-141A ST-3170 FEED-PE-REP-041

A3000-03 · S2 and S3 satellite packages — technology and permits

Envelopes reserved — technology selection and permit HOLD

Reasoning: A CO-in-argon stream and a hydrogen / potassium stream need their own treatment mechanism, outlet and permit; a caustic absorber does not destroy CO or H₂.

Observation: The model reserves AB-3701 (A-700 thermal abatement candidate) with its independent outlet ST-3702 and permissive station CP-3700, and AB-3801 (A-800 specialist reactive-gas package) with ST-3802 / CP-3800; both outlets are "permit HOLD" and their atmospheric interfaces are records only. D-A3000-01 fixes S3 as a hot-end potassium condenser with no water contact and the S3 vent branch to ASME B31.12 (A-800 review); the A-700 and A-800 emergency reliefs (PSV-701, PSV-801) stay independent of treatment. The satellite floor reservation is 23.0 × 2.9 m; package internals and maintenance withdrawal need vendor design.

Required action: Select the S2 thermal treatment and the S3 condenser / hydrogen handling technology on the re-issued A-700 / A-800 balances; obtain the emissions permits for ST-3702 / ST-3802; fix the vendor envelopes.

Evidence: D-A3000-01 AB-3701 ST-3702 CP-3700 AB-3801 ST-3802 CP-3800 IF-A3700-ATM IF-A3800-ATM

A3000-04 · Dry-inventory and KBH₄-feeder vent packages

Selection HOLD — outlets positively blinded

Reasoning: Dry inerted inventories breathe argon with fines and, on the KBH₄ side, hydrolysis-sensitive dust; they must not enter the wet train.

Observation: The model reserves DC-3811 (dry inventory vent) and DC-3812 (KBH₄ feeder vent) as selection-HOLD packages with positively blinded outlets. The previous review corrected the tags: BL-VENT801 is dry-inventory / mixer breathing, BL-REL801 the A-800 emergency relief outlet, BL-RV701 the A-700 relief. Neither package is on LST-001 and neither has a duty or a material.

Required action: Define the breathing and dust loads from the A-700 / A-800 inerting basis (D-A6000-01 gas ruling), select the package type, register both and assign their outlets.

Evidence: DC-3811 DC-3812 IF-A3811-OUTLET IF-A3812-OUTLET MODEL

A3000-05 · Assembled wet train — connections, air gap and liquor handling

Geometry checks pass — six interfaces unresolved

Reasoning: An assembled model with every endpoint dispositioned is the basis for the P&ID and for the plant-wide connectivity checks; the makeup-water air gap is a backflow safeguard that must survive detailing.

Observation: The revision A3000-45 register dispositions 340 endpoints (connected, positively blinded, intentional discharge, intentional air gap); six interfaces remain unresolved — the fan and stack drain receivers, the two external supplies (water, NaOH) and the two dry-package outlets — all positively blinded in the planning configuration. The proposed 520 mm air gap between the water supply and TK-3184 is intentional and must not be bridged; the V-3110 inlet has been re-aligned with an elbow and a straight approach; six pump adapters match their pipe sizes. The checks are re-run for this revision (see recorded checks). Liquor handling — T-3180 with duty / standby P-3181A / B, NaOH day tank T-3182 / P-3182, makeup TK-3184 / P-3184, sealed condensate receiver TK-3111 / P-3111 and controlled blowdown P-3183 — is modelled, but overflow, bypass and blowdown destinations are records only and the drain-seal, receiver-capacity and disposal acceptance remain HOLD.

Required action: Keep the air gap through detailing (final flood level, splash control, overflow duty); assign the fan / stack drain receivers, the water and NaOH supplies and the blowdown, overflow and bypass destinations on the P&ID; carry the A3000-45 register into the P&ID line list.

A3000-06 · Materials of construction

Ruled for the register train — model-only items uncovered

Reasoning: Wet acid gas with caustic liquor sets FRP after the cooling / wetting point and alloy on the hot, dry inlet side.

Observation: D-A3000-MOC applies MAT-002 to the seven register items: vinyl-ester-novolac FRP after cooling / wetting, PP / PVDF packing and demister, 316L or Alloy 601 / 602 CA on the hot / dry sections; MDL-001 (HDPE / XLPE liquor tank, PVDF / PFA-lined pumps) superseded. The model-only items — T-3182, P-3182, ME-3150, TK-3111, P-3111, P-3183, TK-3184, P-3184, HD-3100, CP-3000 and the six satellite items — have neither a register identity nor a material.

Required action: Extend MAT-002 to the model-only wet-train items once they are registered; specify the S2 / S3 package materials with the technology selection (S3 to ASME B31.12).

Evidence: D-A3000-MOC CF-materialOfConstruc-T-3180 CF-materialOfConstruc-P-3181A T-3182 ME-3150 HD-3100 FEED-PE-MAT-002
