The existing prepared snapshot connects geometry, parts, routes, safety checks and measurement references in one object graph. Removing details from its first load without splitting those dependencies could produce incomplete measurements or clearance results.

  1. Split display geometry from the complete engineering record.

  3. Load detailed geometry by selected area or equipment; keep review actions unavailable until their complete data is ready.
  4. Compare route, clearance and measurement results with the full-model baseline before replacing the loader.
