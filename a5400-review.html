Codes and regulations

Researched for the pad position (QFL 2026-10-08). The BC codes found set no minimum distance between a fired heater and a building wall; the position is limited by the tank, containment and vent rules below, by the road and walkway beside the wall, and by separation from A-800 and A-900 (industry practice).

Open items

  • Prevailing wind and the site boundary east of x 140 (stack and burner end orientation).
  • HX-601 duty and nozzle sizing: the DN300 main reduces to DN100 at the HX-601 battery limit; the 715 m³/h package flow would be about 25 m/s in DN100 unless HX-601 takes a small fraction.
  • Oil product, flash point and autoignition temperature (vendor, U8); they fix the Class I treatment (CR-1).
  • CR-4 is met in the model after the V318 pad widening (1.5 m or more from the shells); the vendor tank construction and the final containment volume (largest tank) are still to confirm.
  • Rack span of about 11 m over the rerouted perimeter road for the 0.52 m insulated mains: structural and pipe-stress check.
  • Perimeter road reroute (x 125 → 140 around the pad) is a proposal, not an approved roadway.
  • HAZOP (U8, FEED-PS-HOP-001): burner management, hot-oil release, separation from A-800 and A-900 (CR-9 is practice, not a BC code).
  • Registration of the automated control system design and responsible-person notice (CR-7, owner).
  • Research not done: Gas Safety Regulation and CSA B149.3 field evaluation, rated-wall spatial separation, stack emission permitting (CR-10).
