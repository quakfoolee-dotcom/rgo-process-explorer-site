A800-01 · KBH₄ : rGO ratio and feed-rate basis

Ratio ruled 1 : 1 — rates withdrawn

Reasoning: The reagent ratio and the rGO rate set reagent flow, hydrogen evolution, potassium vapour, furnace duty and every feeder rating in the area.

Observation: D-A800-01 applies the DBM V2.0 / MOC-001 ruling of 1 : 1 KBH₄ : rGO (PFD V5.1.1, REP-019 and REP-022 agree); DAT-008 item 2.6 and the MEB-010 R_KBH4 of 1.5 are superseded, not deleted. The rGO rate is withdrawn: 15.75 kg/h (MEB-010, on 50 kg/h graphite), 25.2 / 37.8 kg/h (DAT-008), 28.07 kg/h (REP-019 / 022) against the DBM V2.0 17 / 22 kg/h rGO (411 / 535 kg/d). With them go the KBH₄ feed rate (37.8 kg/h on the old ratio), hydrogen evolved, the F-801 / F-802 / PY-801 powers and the MEB-010 21.8 kW duty, which omits the ≈ 44 kW reaction heat.

Required action: Revise FEED-PE-DAT-008 (V1.1), FEED-PE-MEB-010 (V9) and Narrative §4.13 / capacity tables to 1 : 1 at the DBM V2.0 rate; restate H₂ evolution, K vapour, off-gas and reagent flows and the feeder ratings.

A800-02 · PY-801 design closure and REP-022 thermal correction

Design basis approved — five RED items open

Reasoning: A driven endothermic reactor is sized on reaction heat and residence, not on the heater allowance of a screening balance.

Observation: D-A800-02 adopts the REP-019 closure as the design-basis statement: one continuous indirectly electrically heated screw-furnace train, 1.10 m ID × 10 m retort, 1.05 m screw at ≈ 0.285 rpm, five zones, 120 kW installed, 2 h at 750 °C under argon; H-803 as a 3.75 m³ mass-flow wedge hopper with a 120 kg reactive-inventory cap; RV-801 not accepted as both metering device and airlock. REP-022 supersedes the thermal basis: ≈ 44 kW endothermic reaction heat at 1 : 1 (five times the 8 kW allowance), so the 120 kW installed duty must be re-checked. Five RED items: ratio (closed), 5 °C/min ramp vs 2 h residence (≈ 4.5 h needed), pressure "a few mbar" vs the +2 psig / −3 mbarg statements, the 585–700 °C molten-salt window, and ≈ 20 kg/h potassium vapour.

Required action: Close the four remaining REP-022 RED items and re-check the installed duty against the reaction heat before freezing the PY-801 package; reconcile the pressure sign and value in the P&ID basis.

Evidence: D-A800-02 GAP-A-800-DF-b6dd98 PY-801 H-803 RV-801 FEED-PE-REP-019 FEED-PE-REP-022

A800-03 · Inert atmosphere, hydrolysis hazard and hazardous area

Approved from DAT-008 — HAC to confirm

Reasoning: KBH₄ hydrolyses with moisture and the furnace evolves hydrogen; the inert envelope and the area classification follow.

Observation: Approved (D-A800-02, DAT-008): ≤ 200 ppm O₂ in the mixer envelope, ≤ 100 ppm H₂O in feed streams and inert gas (KBH₄ hydrolysis hazard), argon ≥ 99.999 % (≤ 2 ppm O₂ at source), Zone 1 / 2 gas plus Zone 21 / 22 dust internal. The model provides the dry-argon regulation manifold AR-801 (2,229 parts), the O₂ / moisture / H₂ verification assembly AIT-801 and the reagent charge lock LK-802 — none on LST-001.

Required action: Confirm the hazardous-area classification on the HAC drawing; define the argon demand of AR-801 with the A-6000 inert-gas ruling (D-A6000-01) and the moisture-control basis of the KBH₄ supply chain (LK-802, H-802).

Evidence: D-A800-02 MX-801 AR-801 AIT-801 LK-802 FEED-PE-DAT-008

A800-04 · Off-gas basis: hydrogen and potassium vapour — S3 segregation

Segregation required — S3 header, K condenser

Reasoning: A hydrogen-rich argon stream carrying potassium vapour must be treated as a continuous process stream with a hot-end condenser, never combined with wet or CO-bearing vents.

Observation: D-A800-02 adopts REP-041: H₂ 12–35 Nm³/h (30–55 vol % in Ar) with 10–20 kg/h potassium vapour — treat H₂ as a continuous process stream and the K / B aerosol as fouling and materials exposure; segregate as system S3 with a hot-end K condenser; PSV-801 and the vent branch to ASME B31.12 (MAT-002 M-054). PSV-801 shares model equipment 109 with PY-801; the relief and residue interfaces have no destinations.

Required action: Confirm S3 segregation and the K condenser on FEED-PFD-3000 and the A-800 P&ID; give PSV-801 its own register identity and set-pressure basis; assign the relief and residue destinations.

Evidence: D-A800-02 GAP-A-800-DF-95870e GAP-A-800-ID-602f13 PSV-801 IF-A800-RELIEF IF-A800-RESIDUE FEED-PE-REP-041

A800-05 · Materials of construction

Ruled — RV-801 not yet covered

Reasoning: Dry KBH₄ and rGO powders demand grounded, electropolished contact surfaces; the furnace and off-gas train see potassium and alkali condensate at temperature.

Observation: D-A800-MOC applies MAT-002: 316L electropolished, grounded and bonded with hard-faced wear locations for H-801 / F-801 / MX-801 / H-803; 316L dry-contact with PTFE / PFA / PVDF for the KBH₄ side H-802 / F-802; Alloy 602 CA or 601 for PY-801; Alloy 601 or Ni 200 / 201 in the hot / alkali-condensing sections of E-801 and FN-801; PSV-801 316 / 316L welded to ASME B31.12. MDL-001 (304L, high-purity alumina retort) superseded. RV-801 has an MDL-001 material only.

Required action: Extend MAT-002 to RV-801; issue the A-800 datasheets on the MAT-002 base case once the ratio / rate revision (A800-01) is in.

A800-06 · Register and model reconciliation

Register reconciliation required

Reasoning: Every equipment item in the area must have one identity in the register, the model and the data layer.

Observation: Nine LST-001 tags (H-801, H-802, F-801, F-802, MX-801, H-803, RV-801, PY-801, E-801, FN-801) are bound to model geometry. The model additionally owns TR-801, LK-802, AR-801, AIT-801, PL-801, BL-800-UTIL, two fire points and two emergency stations without register identities. Design notes name C-801 / C-802 screw feeders where the register, PFD-0800 and the model have the loss-in-weight feeders F-801 / F-802, and an E-802 that is on neither. DAT-008 is unsigned with 14 blank items.

Required action: Register TR-801, LK-802, AR-801, AIT-801, PL-801 and BL-800-UTIL on LST-001; retire the C-801 / C-802 names or map them to F-801 / F-802; rule E-802 in or out; complete and sign DAT-008 on the revised basis.

A800-07 · Access, reactive inventory and fire points

Model access provided — qualification open

Reasoning: Dry-reagent handling on an elevated feed structure needs rated access, a capped reactive inventory and fire points at the approaches.

Observation: The model provides the feeder access and equipment-support structure PL-801 (1,080 parts), fire points at the dry-reagent stair approach (FE-801) and the doping service frontage (FE-802), and two emergency stations. REP-019 caps the H-803 reactive inventory at 120 kg; the KBH₄ charging route through LK-802 and the reagent interface IF-A800-REAGENT are records without a defined supply chain.
