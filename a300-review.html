Findings

A300-01 · Fixing tank T-303 sizing basis

Sizing basis withdrawn — re-issue required

Reasoning: Vessel volume, batch mass and cooling duty must derive from the current design basis and from the chosen dilution / quench arrangement.

Observation: MEB-006 V14 sizes T-303 at 47.6 m³ (N = 2, 34,988 kg batch, 211 kW peak) on the 50 kg/h graphite, 12 h fill and 46 kg/kg H₂SO₄ basis that DBM V2.0 withdrew; REP-037 puts the as-drawn quench at ≈ 280 m³ / ≈ 300 t and 1.7–2.3 MW heat of dilution, or ≈ 65 m³ with in-line cooled dilution (Option B). D-A300-01 withdrew every MEB-006 sizing value and approved only the batch structure — one fixing batch per R-201 batch (DBM V2.0 §3.3). The model vessel (equipment 4) therefore has no approved capacity.

Required action: Decide the Option A / Option B dilution and cooling arrangement, re-issue MEB-006 on DBM V2.0, then size T-303, its cooling and the settle / decant step; bind the result to model equipment 4 through decisions.csv.

A300-02 · Acid-wash vessels T-305 — count and marination hold point

Hold point — vessel count is not a design decision

Reasoning: A vessel count driven by a pilot hold time cannot be frozen until that hold time is a design value.

Observation: MEB-006 sizes T-305 at 59.4 m³ × N = 10 from a 96 h pilot marination flagged HOLD POINT, on the withdrawn batch basis and an unreduced HCl dose (51,050 kg batch). D-A300-01 withdrew the sizing, the count and the 96 h value; LST-001 lists one T-305 and the model carries one (equipment 28). REP-037 notes the pilot 12 h settle is geometry, not chemistry.

Required action: Establish the required marination / wash time by bench program on representative TLGO, then fix N and vessel size on the re-issued MEB-006; reconcile the register count with the model.

Evidence: D-A300-01 GAP-A-300-DF-0e7f71 GAP-A-300-ID-ed2122 FEED-PE-MEB-006

A300-03 · Reagent ratios and temperature envelope

Approved — PFD V5.1.1 and MEB-006 consistent

Reasoning: Dosing and temperature limits are the operating envelope the control narrative and datasheets must carry.

Observation: Approved on the PFD per-kg-Pre-G denominator: 1.875 kg (1.625 L) 50 wt% H₂O₂ and 72.63 kg (67.63 L) 15 wt% HCl per kg Pre-G (D-A300-02); T ≤ 50 °C during HCl addition / transfer (PFD Safety Note 1), 40 °C nominal, H₂O₂ dosing ≤ 40 °C (REP-037 §4), target pH ≈ 1 (D-A300-03). The MEB-006 per-kg-graphite ratios are recorded as superseded, not deleted; the denominator difference is logged as an identity gap.

Required action: Carry the approved envelope into the cause-and-effect matrix and the LST-007 loops for T-303 / T-305 (TIC, dosing permissives, agitator interlock) and into the equipment datasheets; state the denominator on every reissued sheet.

A300-04 · Oxygen evolution, venting and header segregation

Segregation required — critical

Reasoning: A vent carrying evolved oxygen must never share a header with hydrogen-bearing or CO-bearing streams.

Observation: REP-037 records 310–340 Nm³ O₂ per batch (≈ 160 Nm³/h peak) from peroxide decomposition on Mn²⁺ (F-46 Critical). D-A300-04 adopted segregation from the A-3000 header that carries H₂, the PFD open-vent basis (no closed system; vent sized for O₂ and acid mist) and the dosing / agitator interlock. The model represents the destination only as the interface IF-A300-PEROXIDE-VENT; D-A3000-01 rules a separate S1 wet-scrubber system for wet vents.

Required action: Raise the MoC on FEED-PFD-3000 for S1 / S2 / S3 segregation, size the T-303 vent for peak O₂ plus acid mist, and give IF-A300-PEROXIDE-VENT a register identity and destination in the model.

Evidence: D-A300-04 GAP-A-300-DF-81c2b4 IF-A300-PEROXIDE-VENT FEED-PE-REP-037

A300-05 · Materials of construction

Ruled — confirm at datasheet issue

Reasoning: Wetted materials for 50 wt% H₂O₂, 15 wt% HCl and hot acidic slurry govern vessel, pump and centrifuge selection.

Observation: D-A300-MOC applies the plant-wide ruling that FEED-PE-MAT-002 governs: T-303 / T-304 dual-laminate FRP (PVDF/ECTFE liner), C-301 Hastelloy C-276 with WC / ceramic hard-facing, T-301 / P-301 passivated 316L or fluoropolymer, T-302 / P-302 CPVC / HDPE / PVDF. MDL-001 (glass-lined CS, 904L, XLPE) and the REP-037 alternatives (vinyl-ester FRP, titanium bowl) are superseded, not deleted; the uncontrolled A300 V14 datasheet package is not a source. Twelve model records now carry the material.

Required action: Issue the A-300 datasheets on the MAT-002 base case; confirm the T-303 lining against the Option B temperature excursion (heat of dilution) and the C-301 bowl material with the vendor before purchase.

Evidence: D-A300-MOC CF-materialOfConstruc-T-303 CF-materialOfConstruc-C-301 GAP-A-300-DF-b717a4 GAP-A-300-ID-350a94 FEED-PE-MAT-002

A300-06 · Decanter C-301 and centrate handling

Capacity basis open

Reasoning: The decanter feed rate and centrate route depend on the re-issued batch basis and on the wash-water case.

Observation: C-301, T-304 and P-306 have model geometry (equipment 30, 31, pump 539999601) and a ruled material, but no rated feed or solids capture value exists in any controlled source; MEB-006 stream 309/310 rates sit on the withdrawn basis. Stream numbering 305–317 (LST-001) is recorded as an identity item. In the assembled model C-301 sits on an elevated frame above T-304 (image 04); the washed-cake route to A-400 (stream 313/314) is not among A-300-owned parts — it may be owned by A-400 or unmodelled, which the isolated image cannot decide.

Required action: Derive the C-301 feed rate, solids loading and centrate flow from the re-issued MEB-006; obtain vendor test data on representative slurry; confirm the T-304 → A-1000 acidic-centrate route and its wastewater case (REP-038).

Evidence: C-301 T-304 P-306 GAP-A-300-ID-995a42 FEED-PE-LST-001

A300-07 · Register and model reconciliation

Register reconciliation required

Reasoning: Every equipment item in the area must have one identity in the register, the model and the data layer.

Observation: All 12 LST-001 tags are bound to model geometry. Three items named in design notes are absent from both register and model: agitators AG-303 / AG-305 and the Option B cooler E-303 (REP-037). Conversely the model owns five containment bunds BND-301 … BND-305 (9.3–39.9 m³ retention), fire point FE-301 and four emergency stations (ES-301, ES-300-05/06/17) that have no register identity; the T-303 jacket utility is recorded only as U301 / U302 "from CWS / to CWR" in LST-001.

Required action: Add AG-303, AG-305 and E-303 (subject to Option B) to LST-001 and the model; assign register tags to the bunds, fire point and stations or record them as model-side safety assets by decision; confirm the T-303 jacket utility against the A-5000 review.
