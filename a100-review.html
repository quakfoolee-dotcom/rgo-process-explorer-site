Findings

A100-01 · Graphite feed basis

Basis withdrawn — DBM V2.0 rate to be issued

Reasoning: Premix duty, acid flow, dust load and pump ratings all scale with the graphite rate, which must be single-valued on the DBM V2.0 basis.

Observation: Two rates are on record: 50 kg/h (MEB-002 V8) and 80 kg/h (LST-005); D-A100-01 withdrew both in favour of the DBM V2.0 design rate of 63.5 kg/h (1,523 kg/d), and with them the P-101 / P-102 / SF-101 powers and the T-102 heat-trace duty. Approved on MEB-002 (D-A100-02): 18.4 kg 98 wt% H₂SO₄ per kg graphite (MEB-002, MEB-003 and LST-005 agree), 0.7 % vent loss on FG-103, 99 % DC-101 collection; and the DBM V2.0 batch basis — premix feeds R-141 batches of 500 kg graphite.

Required action: Re-issue FEED-PE-MEB-002 (V9) and LST-005 on DBM V2.0 (1,523 kg/d graphite design); then derive the R-101 duty, acid and premix flows and the pump and feeder ratings.

Evidence: D-A100-01 D-A100-02 CF-graphiteFeedKgh-R-101 GAP-A-100-DF-5b7d93 GAP-A-100-ID-1ba2e8 FEED-PE-MEB-002 FEED-PE-LST-005 FEED-PE-DBM-001

A100-02 · Graphite charging — H-101 / SF-101 identity

Register reconciliation required

Reasoning: The register lists a hopper and a feeder; the model carries them as one charging station, so the two tags need one agreed identity before values can attach.

Observation: LST-001 and MAT-002 M-002 list hopper H-101 and feeder SF-101; the model owns a single "Graphite charging" record H-101 / SF-101 (equipment 38, 150 parts) with geometry-only entries for each tag. Materials are ruled for both (304L / 316L, conductive and grounded), but the compound record cannot carry either tag's approvals until the identity is split or aliased. Dust collector DC-101 and transfer pump P-102 are on the register with no model geometry; the model's P-PG101 premix distribution pump and P-101 dosing pump carry no register identity.

Required action: Split H-101 / SF-101 into two records (or record the alias by decision), model DC-101 and P-102 or record them as unmodelled by decision, and add P-101 / P-PG101 to LST-001.

Evidence: GAP-A-100-ID-b3e1bf GAP-A-100-ID-4aa09e H-101-SF-101 H-101 SF-101 DC-101 P-102 P-101

A100-03 · Materials of construction

Ruled — P-101 not yet covered

Reasoning: 98 wt% sulphuric acid with graphite solids sets the vessel, tank and pump materials.

Observation: D-A100-MOC applies MAT-002: R-101 glass-lined carbon steel (ISO 28721 / DIN 28060); T-102 lined carbon steel or high-silicon austenitic stainless; P-102 PFA / ETFE-lined sealless with SiC bushings; H-101 / SF-101 / DC-101 304L / 316L conductive and grounded. MDL-001 (XLPE / HDPE storage tank) superseded. P-101 has an MDL-001 material only.

Required action: Extend MAT-002 to P-101 (and P-PG101 once registered); issue A-100 datasheets on the MAT-002 base case after the rate revision.

Evidence: D-A100-MOC CF-materialOfConstruc-T-102 GAP-A-100-DF-70a7d2 FEED-PE-MAT-002

A100-04 · Containment, stations and fire point

Model provision — register identities open

Reasoning: Acid storage and premix need retention, emergency stations and a fire point at the pedestrian approach.

Observation: The model owns two bunds — BND-101 sulphuric-acid storage (11.5 m³ retention) and BND-102 graphite / acid premix (20.2 m³) — two emergency stations ES-101 / ES-102 and fire point FE-101 at the premix pedestrian approach; none is on LST-001. Retention volumes are model annotations, not verified containment calculations.

Required action: Assign register identities to the bunds, stations and fire point or record them as model-side safety assets by decision; verify the retention volumes in the plant-wide containment review.

Evidence: GAP-A-100-ID-ac8908 ES-101 ES-102 MODEL
