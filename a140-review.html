A140-01 · Reactor count, batch and cycle basis

DBM V2.0 basis approved — sizing withdrawn

Reasoning: The number of reactors, the graphite per batch and the cycle time are the area's governing statement; vessel size and duties follow them.

Observation: D-A140-01 approves the DBM V2.0 basis — 500 kg graphite per R-141 batch (batch sizes of PFD V5.1.1), 2.3 batches/day nominal and 3.0 design, two operating reactors plus one spare (rule C-05) — and withdraws the MEB-003 V16 / LST-005 sizing (80 kg/h, 543 kg per batch, 7 h cycle, 3 batches/day; 6.12 / 8.16 m³ vs LST-005 7.87 / 10.49 m³ vs REP-040 5.4 / 6.8 m³), the batch masses, the 593.6 kg Pre-G per batch (DBM: 550 kg per R-201 batch) and every thermal duty. The cycle is 7 h (MEB-003) vs 10 h (PFD / Narrative) vs 9–10 h (REP-040 with the cool-down leg removed) and follows the hot-transfer decision. The model shows four reactors A–D; the DBM count is 2 + 1.

Required action: Re-issue FEED-PE-MEB-003 (V17) on DBM V2.0 — 500 kg per batch, 2 + 1 reactors, 9–10 h cycle with P₂O₅ on heat-up and hot transfer — and reconcile the model's fourth reactor with the register. The vessel size is now proposed by FEED-PE-DAT-018 (8.0 m³, A140-06) and awaits a decision.

A140-02 · Reaction envelope, P₂O₅ on heat-up and hot transfer

Approved — MoC on PFD sheets 0140 / 0160 required

Reasoning: Temperature, hold time, reagent ratios and the transfer condition define the synthesis and the utility demand; the hot-transfer decision removes a cooling leg and the chilled-water need.

Observation: Approved (D-A140-02, MEB-003 / REP-040): 80 °C hold for 4.5 h; 18.4 kg H₂SO₄, 0.50 kg K₂S₂O₈ and 0.50 kg P₂O₅ per kg graphite; P₂O₅ dosed during heat-up so its ≈ 400 MJ supplies ≈ 45 % of the heat-up duty; hot transfer at 80 °C to T-161 (PFD 10–20 °C superseded); utility basis HWS 95 °C / CW 25–30 °C with chilled water no longer needed once the cool-down leg is removed. REP-040 halves the 317 kW heat-up duty with P₂O₅ credit; the 83.8 kW jacket cooling rests on an assumed 50 kW exotherm.

Required action: Raise the MoC on FEED-PE-PFD-001 sheets 0140 / 0160 for hot transfer and P₂O₅-on-heat-up; confirm T-161 water inlet ≤ 28 °C keeps T-161 below 50 °C (REP-042); carry the envelope into LST-007 and the C&E matrix.

A140-03 · Persulfate exotherm and temperature safeguards

Safety statement adopted — calorimetry required

Reasoning: A persulfate / sulphuric-acid / graphite batch is a runaway-capable reactive system; the safeguard ladder needs a measured exotherm behind it.

Observation: D-A140-02 adopts REP-040's worst credible case — +54 K adiabatic rise to ≈ 135 °C if the whole reactive inventory releases; hazards chemical, not pressure — as the safety design statement pending calorimetry, and records the 82 / 85 / 88 / 90 °C interlock ladder as a candidate of unstated origin. D-A140-01 withdrew the 83.8 kW jacket cooling capacity (assumed 50 kW exotherm) and the bounded 90–420 MJ persulfate oxidation as unmeasured (REP-040 F-06).

Required action: Run persulfate calorimetry on the design recipe (F-06); confirm the ladder and the jacket capacity in HAZOP / LOPA; record the origin of each setpoint.

Evidence: D-A140-02 D-A140-01 R-141A R-141A-JACKET FEED-PE-REP-040

A140-04 · Reagent charging — CH-141 / CH-142 identity and materials

Register reconciliation required

Reasoning: The register names two tanks and two feeders; the model carries one charging-station record, so materials and duties cannot attach until the identity is agreed.

Observation: LST-001 and MAT-002 M-005 list T-141, T-142, SF-141 and SF-142 (316L dry-contact with PFA / PTFE-lined drop tubes, ruled by D-A140-MOC); the model owns one "Dedicated reagent charging stations" record CH-141 / CH-142 (equipment 43, 1,200 parts) with a geometry-only CH-141 — in the assembled model this is two charging hoppers with feeders on top of each reactor, eight in all (image 01), not two shared stations. P-141 is ruled PFA / ETFE-lined sealless with SiC bearings; the model's P-PG141 synthesis collection pump has no register identity. The four reactor jackets are provisional model subassemblies without register tags; the two emergency stations are model-only.

Required action: Split CH-141 / CH-142 into the four register tags or record the alias by decision; add P-PG141 to LST-001 or map it to P-141; decide whether jackets stay subassemblies.

A140-05 · Independent containment and access

Model provision — retention unverified

Reasoning: Each reactive-synthesis reactor is bunded independently so that one release cannot reach another.

Observation: The model owns four independent bunds BND-141A–D (16.4 m³ retention each), two emergency stations (ES-141, ES-140-03) and fire point FE-141 at the synthesis approach; none is on LST-001. The reactors are glass-lined carbon steel with glass-lined agitator and wetted internals (MAT-002, D-A140-MOC). Retention volumes are model annotations; the hot-transfer decision changes the liquor temperature the bunds may receive.

Required action: Verify retention against the 500 kg batch inventory at 80 °C in the containment review; assign register identities to bunds, stations and fire point or record them by decision.

Preliminary datasheets issued — candidate values, none approved

Reasoning: R-141 design pressure and temperature, the reagent hopper volumes, both feeder rates and the P-141 pump type, flow and head were unrecorded; the R-141 volumes were in open conflict with every source withdrawn.

Observation: FEED-PE-CAL-015 V1.0 sizes A-140 on the bases approved in review 2026-09-22 (C1 three reactors — 2 operating + 1 spare, D-A140-01, R-141D position held; C2 8.0 m³ nominal; C3 reagent hoppers one day + one batch): 10.2 t batch, 9 h cycle giving 5.3 batches/d against 3.05 needed, jacket heating 148 kW on HWS so the P₂O₅-assisted heat-up takes 0.9 h. Datasheets FEED-PE-DAT-018…023 carry it — R-141A/B/C 5.4 / 8.0 m³ glass-lined, 6 barg / FV, 150 °C (above the 135 °C worst case); T-141 1.2 m³; T-142 2.0 m³ under dry gas; SF-141 125 / 150 kg/h; SF-142 200 kg/h with a hard 240 kg/h (4 kg/min) cap; P-141 12 m³/h at 12 m for hot transfer. They enter as 213 candidate rows — none approved.
